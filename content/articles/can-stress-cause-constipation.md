Can stress cause constipation? Yes. Stress shifts your body out of rest-and-digest mode, which can slow how food moves through your gut. Many people also hold tension in their belly and pelvic floor without realizing it, and a braced body doesn't let go easily, including in the bathroom. Some people's guts speed up under stress. Others shut down.

Maybe you notice it on trips, during busy seasons, or when you're running on empty. You eat the same way, but everything slows down.

## Digestion needs your body to feel safe

Your gut works best when your nervous system is in "rest and digest" mode. That's when your body sends energy to digestion and keeps things moving.

When your nervous system senses stress, it pulls energy away from digestion. For some people, that slows the gut and leads to constipation.

## Why stress speeds some people up and slows others down

Your nervous system has different ways of protecting you. Fight or flight can speed up the colon, which is why some people get [diarrhea when they're stressed](/articles/can-stress-cause-diarrhea). But when stress turns into overwhelm or shutdown, things can slow down instead. Many people with IBS switch between the two.

## A braced body holds on

Many people unknowingly clench their belly, pelvic floor, and glutes when they're stressed. Those are the same muscles that need to relax to have a bowel movement. If they stay tight, going can feel difficult or incomplete.

That bracing is a protective habit your nervous system learned, not a willpower problem.

## Signs your constipation may be stress-related

- It's worse during stressful seasons or when traveling
- You can't go in unfamiliar or rushed situations
- You often feel tense in your belly, hips, or pelvis
- It eases on calm days or vacations
- Your doctor hasn't found another cause

Constipation can also come from diet, dehydration, medications, thyroid problems, and other conditions. Please see a doctor if it's new, lasts more than a few weeks, or comes with blood, severe pain, or weight loss.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause constipation?** Yes. Anxiety and stress can slow digestion and keep pelvic muscles tense.

**Why do I get constipated when I travel?** New places, schedules, and bathrooms can keep your nervous system on alert, and it may not let your body relax enough to go.

**Can stress cause both constipation and diarrhea?** Yes, many people go back and forth. See [IBS and the nervous system](/articles/ibs-and-the-nervous-system).
