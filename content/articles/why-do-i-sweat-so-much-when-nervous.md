Why do I sweat so much when I'm nervous? Sweating is controlled by your sympathetic nervous system, the fight-or-flight side. Besides cooling you down, your body has a second kind of sweating, sometimes called emotional sweating, that's set off by stress, fear, and embarrassment. It shows up mostly on your palms, soles, underarms, and face. Some people have an overactive version called hyperhidrosis, where sweating is much heavier than the body needs.

You might get clammy hands before shaking someone's hand, sweat through your shirt in a meeting, or feel sweat on your face when you're put on the spot.

## See a doctor if…

Heavy sweating can come from hyperhidrosis, thyroid problems, low blood sugar, infections, menopause, medications, and other conditions. Please see a doctor if you sweat heavily all over without a clear reason, sweat at night, lose weight without trying, or if sweating started suddenly. Get emergency care for sweating with chest pain, shortness of breath, or nausea, which can be signs of a heart attack.

## How your nervous system controls sweat

Your sweat glands are switched on by sympathetic nerves. When your body senses danger, it gets you ready to act, and sweaty palms may have once helped grip and climb. That's why nervous sweat often starts in your hands and feet before the rest of your body.

Worrying about sweating adds more stress signals, so many people get stuck in a loop.

## What research shows

A review on hyperhidrosis in *Thoracic Surgery Clinics* describes it as an overactive response of the sympathetic nerves to the sweat glands, most often in the palms, soles, and underarms. It often starts in childhood or the teen years and runs in families. Emotional stress is a common trigger for episodes, though the glands themselves are normal.

## Signs nerves are behind your sweating

- You sweat more in stressful or social situations
- Your palms and underarms sweat most
- You don't sweat heavily while sleeping
- Worrying about sweating makes it worse

## Common questions

**Why do my hands sweat when I'm anxious?** Your palms have many sweat glands that respond to stress signals from your nervous system.

**Is it normal to sweat a lot when nervous?** Some nervous sweating is normal. If it's heavy and affects your daily life, talk with a doctor about hyperhidrosis treatments.

**What helps nervous sweating?** Clinical-strength antiperspirant, breathable clothes, and calming your breathing can help. A doctor can offer more options.
