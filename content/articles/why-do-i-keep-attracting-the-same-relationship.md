Different person. Same story.

Maybe it's the partner who pulls away. The one who needs fixing. The one who's hot and cold. The one who makes you feel like you have to earn their attention.

You've been manifesting a loving, steady relationship. So why do you keep ending up in the same one?

## Your nervous system is drawn to familiar

Your nervous system doesn't choose what feels good. It chooses what feels familiar, because familiar is what it already knows how to survive.

If love in your early life came with distance, unpredictability, criticism, or having to earn it, your body may have learned that that's what love feels like. So when someone shows up who matches that feeling, there's an instant pull. It feels like chemistry.

And when someone calm, consistent, and safe shows up? Your body might call it boring. Or it might not trust it.

## How it shows up

- You feel a strong spark with people who aren't available
- Steady, kind partners feel boring or "too nice"
- You end up chasing, waiting, or proving yourself
- You feel anxious when things are going well
- You pull away when someone gets close
- The relationships change, but the feeling stays the same

## Why manifesting love can feel blocked

If closeness once felt unsafe, your body may still brace against it, even while your heart wants it. You can ask for love all day long, but if your nervous system doesn't feel safe receiving it, it may keep steering you back to what it knows.
