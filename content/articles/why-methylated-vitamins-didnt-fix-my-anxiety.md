Why didn't methylated vitamins fix your anxiety? Because anxiety is driven mainly by your nervous system, not by your folate level. Vitamins can support your body's chemistry, and some people feel a little better on them. But if your nervous system learned that it has to stay on guard, no supplement can change that rule. It keeps bracing, and the anxiety keeps coming back.

Maybe you found out about your MTHFR variant and it felt like a relief. Finally, an explanation. You switched to methylated vitamins, cut out folic acid, and waited.

Maybe it helped a little for a few weeks. Maybe you felt worse. Maybe nothing changed at all. Now you're wondering if you need a different brand, a higher dose, or a new protocol.

## The gene was probably never the whole story

MTHFR variants are very common, and people who have them can still process folate. Research shows only a small link between MTHFR and depression, and the link with anxiety is unclear. You can read more in [MTHFR, anxiety, and depression](/articles/mthfr-anxiety-depression).

So if you've corrected your vitamins and still feel anxious, you didn't fail. You were treating one small piece of a much bigger picture.

## What anxiety really is

Anxiety is your nervous system trying to protect you. It senses danger, often [before you're even aware of it](/articles/what-is-neuroception), and it gets your body ready to respond: faster heart, tight chest, racing thoughts, knotted stomach.

That system learns from experience. If it learned early in life that you had to stay alert, that mistakes weren't safe, or that you had to hold everything together, it keeps running that rule. A vitamin doesn't reach that rule. It's not a nutrient problem. It's a learned pattern.

## Why the first few weeks can feel better

A lot of people notice a short lift when they start a new protocol. That's real. Feeling like you're finally doing something can bring hope and relief, and your body can respond to that.

But if the underlying pattern is still running, the anxiety usually creeps back. It's the same thing that happens with [affirmations that stop working](/articles/why-affirmations-dont-work): the surface changes, and the rule underneath stays the same.

## Signs your anxiety is a nervous system pattern

- It rises and falls with stress, not with your supplements
- It shows up in certain situations or around certain people
- You feel calmer on vacation, on the same vitamins
- You can't [talk yourself out of it](/articles/why-cant-i-talk-myself-out-of-anxiety), even when you know it doesn't make sense
- It started or got worse during a hard season of life

Never stop or start medication or supplements without talking to your doctor. If anxiety is severe or you're thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988.
