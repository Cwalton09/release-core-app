Can emotions cause physical pain? Emotions can create real physical sensations and can make pain worse. Brain research shows that social pain, like rejection and heartbreak, uses some of the same brain areas as physical pain. That's why a breakup can make your chest ache, and why grief or loneliness can make your whole body hurt. The pain isn't imagined. Your nervous system is processing it as pain.

You might feel a heavy ache in your chest, a tight throat, a sore stomach, or flare-ups of old pain during emotional times.

## See a doctor if…

Physical pain should always be checked, even if you're going through something emotional. Call 911 for chest pain with shortness of breath, sweating, or pain spreading to your arm, jaw, or back. Please see a doctor for pain that is new, severe, or doesn't go away.

## What research shows

A 2011 study in the *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* asked people who had recently gone through an unwanted breakup to look at a photo of their ex. Brain areas that process the physical sensation of pain became active, overlapping with the areas that lit up when the same people felt physical pain. The authors concluded that rejection and physical pain share a common representation in the brain.

An earlier study in *Science* found that being left out of a simple online game activated brain areas also involved in physical pain.

## Why emotional pain feels physical

Your nervous system's job is to protect you. For humans, being rejected or alone has always been dangerous. So the brain treats emotional hurt as a threat, using the same alarm it uses for physical injury. When that alarm keeps going, your body can tighten, ache, and become more sensitive to pain. ## Common questions

**Can sadness cause body aches?** Yes. Sadness and grief can make your body ache and make existing pain feel worse.

**Why does my chest hurt when I'm upset?** Strong emotions tighten muscles and activate the stress response. Always get chest pain checked.

**Can holding in emotions cause pain?** Many people find pain eases when they let feelings move. Your body stays tense when it's bracing.
