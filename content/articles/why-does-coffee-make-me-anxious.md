Why does coffee make me anxious? Caffeine speeds up your nervous system. It raises alertness, heart rate, and the same body signals you feel during anxiety. For people whose nervous system is already on alert, those signals can tip into anxiety or even panic. Research shows caffeine triggered panic attacks in about half of people with panic disorder, but rarely in people without it.

You might notice jitters, a racing heart, shaky hands, or a sense of dread after coffee, energy drinks, or pre-workout.

## When to get help

If caffeine causes chest pain, fainting, or an irregular heartbeat, see a doctor. If you have frequent panic attacks, talk to your doctor or a therapist. Panic disorder is very treatable.

## What research shows

A systematic review and meta-analysis in *General Hospital Psychiatry* looked at placebo-controlled caffeine studies. In people with panic disorder, 51% had a panic attack after caffeine and none had one after placebo. Only about 2% of healthy people did. Caffeine also raised anxiety in healthy adults, but at high doses, roughly equal to five cups of coffee.

So caffeine isn't the root cause. It turns up a nervous system that's already sensitive.

## Why some people feel it more

- **An alert nervous system.** If your body is already on guard, caffeine pushes it further.
- **Body signals feel like danger.** A faster heartbeat can set off worry, which makes it race more.
- **Poor sleep.** Tired bodies react more strongly.
- **Genetics.** People break down caffeine at different speeds.

## Common questions

**Can coffee cause anxiety attacks?** It can trigger them, especially in people with panic disorder.

**Should I quit coffee if I have anxiety?** Many people feel better cutting back. Taper slowly to avoid withdrawal headaches.

**Why didn't coffee used to make me anxious?** A more stressed or alert nervous system can make you more sensitive than you used to be.
