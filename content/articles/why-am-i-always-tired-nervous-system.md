You sleep, and you still wake up tired. You get through the day on coffee and willpower. By evening, you're completely drained, but somehow still too wired to rest.

If you're tired all the time, your nervous system may be working a lot harder than you realize.

## Staying on alert is exhausting

Your nervous system is built to handle stress in short bursts. Something happens, your body gears up, and when it's over, it rests and recovers.

But when your nervous system stays on alert, it never gets that recovery time. It keeps scanning, bracing, and preparing, all day long. That takes a huge amount of energy, even when you're sitting still.

## Wired and tired at the same time

One of the most common signs is feeling wired and exhausted at the same time. Your body is too tired to do much, but too on edge to fully rest.

That's often what burnout feels like from the inside: a nervous system that's been running on high for so long it's running out of fuel.

## Signs your fatigue is connected to your nervous system

- You wake up tired no matter how long you sleep
- You crash in the afternoon or evening
- Rest doesn't feel restful
- You feel exhausted after social situations or busy days
- Small tasks feel like a lot
- You feel flat, foggy, or far away when you're overwhelmed

## Rule out other causes

Ongoing fatigue can come from many things, including thyroid problems, anemia, sleep apnea, vitamin deficiencies, and other health conditions. Please talk to your doctor and get checked, especially if your fatigue is new or getting worse.

If you've rested and it hasn't helped, it's often because the part of you that's on alert never actually got the message that it's safe to stop. You can read more about [being stuck in survival mode](/articles/nervous-system-stuck-in-survival-mode).
