Can stress cause seizures? Stress is one of the most commonly reported seizure triggers for people with epilepsy. It doesn't cause epilepsy, but it can make seizures more likely. Stress is also closely linked to functional seizures, which look like epileptic seizures but come from how the brain and nervous system are functioning, not from abnormal electrical activity.

If you or someone you love has seizures, you've probably noticed they can cluster during hard weeks.

## Medical care comes first

Seizures always need medical evaluation. Call 911 if a seizure lasts more than five minutes, someone doesn't wake up afterward, has trouble breathing, is injured, or it's their first seizure. Never stop or change seizure medication without your doctor.

## Stress and epilepsy

People with epilepsy often name stress as their top trigger. In one study where people logged their seizures with a smartwatch app, stress was the most common trigger they reported, tied to 37% of seizures, followed by missed sleep.

Stress can lower the seizure threshold by changing sleep, hormones, and brain activity. It also often leads to missed sleep and missed meals, which are triggers too.

## Functional seizures

Functional seizures, sometimes called psychogenic nonepileptic seizures or PNES, are real seizures. They're involuntary, and they're not faked. But they come from how the brain and nervous system are working, often in response to stress, trauma, or overwhelm, rather than from epileptic electrical activity.

They're diagnosed by a specialist, often with video EEG, and they're treatable, often with specialized therapy. Many people with functional seizures have a history of significant stress or trauma, which is a strong example of how the nervous system can react to overwhelm in very physical ways.

## Signs stress may be affecting your seizures

- Seizures cluster during stressful periods
- You notice more seizures after poor sleep or conflict
- You feel on edge or overwhelmed before seizures
- Your doctor has mentioned functional seizures

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause seizures?** Anxiety can trigger seizures in people with epilepsy, and stress is closely linked to functional seizures. Any seizure needs a doctor's evaluation.

**Are functional seizures real?** Yes. They're involuntary and real, and they deserve proper diagnosis and care.

**Can stress cause a first seizure?** Stress alone doesn't usually cause epilepsy, but a first seizure always needs urgent medical evaluation.
