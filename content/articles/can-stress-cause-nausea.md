Can stress cause nausea? Yes, stress and anxiety can cause nausea in many people. Your stomach and brain are closely connected through the vagus nerve and stress hormones. When your nervous system shifts into fight-or-flight, digestion slows down and your stomach becomes more sensitive. That can leave you queasy, with no appetite, or feeling like you might throw up.

You might notice it before a hard conversation, on the way to work, first thing in the morning, or when you're waiting on news. It can come with a knot or "pit" in your stomach.

## See a doctor if…

Nausea has many causes, including infections, pregnancy, medications, migraines, and stomach conditions. Please see a doctor if nausea lasts more than a few days, keeps coming back, or comes with weight loss, severe belly pain, vomiting blood, black stools, a high fever, or signs of dehydration. Get urgent care for nausea with chest pain or shortness of breath.

## How stress reaches your stomach

When your body senses danger, it moves energy away from digestion and toward your muscles and heart. Your stomach can empty more slowly, and the normal waves that move food along can change. That's part of why food can sit heavy and turn your stomach when you're stressed.

Stress also makes the nerves in your gut more sensitive, so normal sensations can feel queasy or uncomfortable.

## What research shows

A 2023 review in *The Journal of Physiology* found that both short-term and long-term stress can change how the stomach and gut move, how sensitive they are, and how they work overall.

Animal studies have shown that acute stress slows how fast the stomach empties, through stress signals from the brain and the fight-or-flight nerves. In people, slow stomach emptying is linked with nausea, fullness, and bloating.

## Signs stress may be part of your nausea

- It's worse on stressful days or before certain events
- It's strongest in the morning or when you wake up worried
- Tests haven't found another cause
- It comes with a racing heart, tight chest, or shaky feeling
- It eases once the stressful thing is over

## Common questions

**Can nerves make you feel sick?** Yes. "Nervous stomach" is real. Your gut responds to the same stress signals that make your heart race.

**How do I stop stress nausea fast?** Slow breathing, sipping water, fresh air, and small bland snacks can help. Ginger helps some people. If it keeps happening, talk with your doctor.

**Can anxiety nausea last all day?** It can come and go through the day, especially during long stretches of stress.
