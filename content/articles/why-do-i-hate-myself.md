Why do you hate yourself? Usually because your nervous system learned something about you long before you could question it. If you were criticized, ignored, blamed, or made to feel like a problem, your body may have concluded *something is wrong with me*. Self-hatred is that old conclusion still playing. It isn't the truth about who you are.

If you're in a dark place right now and thinking about hurting yourself, please call or text 988 in the U.S. any time, day or night. You don't have to wait until it gets worse.

## Self-hatred is learned

No one is born hating themselves. Babies don't think they're too much or not enough. Those beliefs get learned, usually from how we were treated when we were young and had no other way to make sense of it.

A child can't think *my parent is overwhelmed*. A child thinks *I must be bad*. That's how the belief starts.

## What it can look like

- A harsh inner voice that criticizes everything you do
- Turning on yourself after every mistake
- Feeling like you don't deserve good things
- Not being able to accept compliments
- [Feeling like a failure](/articles/why-do-i-feel-like-a-failure), even when things go well
- Feeling like people would be better off without you

## Where it comes from

Self-hatred often traces back to criticism, neglect, bullying, abuse, or being blamed for things that weren't your fault. Your nervous system may carry rules like:

- *Something is wrong with me*
- *I'm too much*
- *I'm not lovable*
- *If I'm hard on myself, I'll be safe*

That last one matters. Sometimes the inner critic is trying to protect you, by getting to you before anyone else can. See [core beliefs examples](/articles/core-beliefs-examples) and [why do I feel like a burden?](/articles/why-do-i-feel-like-a-burden)

## When to get help

If self-hatred comes with hopelessness, thoughts of hurting yourself, or feeling like you can't go on, please reach out now. Call or text 988 in the U.S., or talk with a doctor or therapist. Release Core is for self-awareness and nervous system support, and it isn't a replacement for crisis or mental health care.

## Common questions

**Why do I hate myself for no reason?** There's usually an old reason. Your body is still following a belief it learned when you were young.

**Why do I hate myself after I make a mistake?** If mistakes once meant getting in trouble or losing love, your body treats them as dangerous.

**Can you stop hating yourself?** Yes. A belief that was learned can change, especially when you work with the part of you that still believes it.

## Why it keeps coming back

When self-hatred keeps returning, your nervous system is still following an old conclusion about who you are.

The real root is often very different from what you think. It usually isn't the mistake you made today or what someone just said. It's an old rule your body learned long before, like *something is wrong with me* or *I'm not lovable*, and today's moment is just turning up its volume.

That's why you do a session. In a Release Core session, your body shows you the exact rule it's still following and where it started.
