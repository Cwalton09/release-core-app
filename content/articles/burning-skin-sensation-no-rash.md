Why does your skin burn with no rash? A burning or painful skin feeling with nothing visible usually means the nerves are involved. It can come from medical causes like shingles, nerve damage, vitamin deficiencies, or diabetes, so it's important to get checked. It can also happen when your nervous system becomes sensitized, turning up pain and touch signals so that normal touch, clothing, or water feels like burning.

Your shirt feels like sandpaper. A light touch stings. Your skin feels sunburned, but it looks completely normal.

## See a doctor

Please get checked, especially if the burning is new, on one side of your body, or comes with numbness, weakness, or a rash that appears later. Burning skin can be an early sign of shingles, which needs prompt treatment. Ask your doctor about:

- Shingles
- Nerve conditions, including neuropathy
- B12 and other vitamin levels
- Blood sugar
- Thyroid function
- Skin conditions and medication side effects

## How a sensitized nervous system makes skin hurt

Your skin's nerves send signals to your spinal cord and brain, and your brain decides how much it hurts. When your nervous system has been on high alert for a long time, it can become sensitized. Researchers call this central sensitization.

In that state, the volume on pain and touch gets turned up. Normal sensations can feel painful, and mild discomfort can feel intense. When normal touch feels painful, doctors call it allodynia.

You can read more in [how the nervous system shapes pain](/articles/how-the-nervous-system-shapes-pain).

## Why stress makes it worse

Stress keeps your nervous system scanning for danger, and sensations in your body get more attention. Stress hormones and inflammation can also affect how reactive your nerves are. So burning skin often flares during hard seasons and settles when you feel safer.

## Signs your nervous system may be part of it

- The burning comes and goes with stress
- It moves around or covers large areas
- Medical tests haven't found a clear cause
- You also have other sensitivity, like to noise, light, or pain
- You feel wired, on edge, or exhausted

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause a burning sensation on the skin?** Many people with anxiety report it, and a sensitized nervous system can amplify skin sensations. Rule out medical causes first.

**Why does my skin hurt when I touch it?** Skin pain from light touch often means the nerves are more sensitive than normal. A doctor can help find out why.

**Is burning skin a sign of shingles?** It can be. Shingles often starts with burning or tingling before a rash appears. See a doctor quickly.
