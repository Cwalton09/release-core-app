Can stress cause memory loss? Yes, stress can make you more forgetful. Stress hormones like cortisol affect the hippocampus, the part of your brain that stores new memories, and the prefrontal cortex, which helps you focus and hold information in mind. When your nervous system is on alert, your brain puts scanning for danger ahead of filing away where you left your keys. For most people, stress-related forgetfulness improves as stress comes down.

You might lose words mid-sentence, forget what you just read, miss appointments, or walk into a room and stand there wondering why you came in.

## See a doctor if…

Please see a doctor if memory problems are getting steadily worse, if you get lost in familiar places, if others are worried about your memory, or if it comes with confusion, personality changes, or trouble with everyday tasks. Thyroid problems, low B12, sleep apnea, depression, medications, and other conditions can also affect memory. Get emergency care for sudden confusion or memory loss, especially with weakness, numbness, or trouble speaking.

## How stress affects memory

When you're stressed, your body releases cortisol and adrenaline. In short bursts, they can actually help you remember something important, like what happened during an emergency. But they also pull your attention toward threat and away from everything else.

When stress goes on for weeks or months, cortisol keeps acting on the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex. A 2009 review in *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* describes how long-term stress affects these brain areas across the lifespan, and how that shows up as changes in memory and thinking.

## What research shows

A 2017 meta-analysis in *Psychological Bulletin* combined 113 studies with more than 6,000 people. Stress just before or while trying to remember made recall worse. Stress before or while learning something usually made it harder to remember later, unless the information was directly tied to the stressful event.

In everyday life, that means stress makes it harder to pull up names, words, and plans when you need them.

## Signs stress may be behind your forgetfulness

- It got worse during a hard season
- You forget small things but remember important events
- You also have trouble focusing, sleeping, or relaxing
- It improves on calmer days or on vacation
- Your mind goes blank under pressure

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause memory loss?** Anxiety can make it hard to focus and remember, because so much of your attention goes to worry and scanning for danger.

**Is stress memory loss permanent?** For most people, no. Memory and focus usually improve as stress and sleep get better.

**Why am I so forgetful lately?** Stress, poor sleep, and a full mental load are common reasons. If it's getting worse or others notice, see a doctor.
