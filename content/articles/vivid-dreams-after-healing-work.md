Why are you having vivid dreams after healing work? Because emotional work gives your brain more to process while you sleep. During REM sleep, the stage when most vivid dreams happen, the brain works through emotional memories and softens their charge. After a session that stirs something old, your nights often get busier for a while.

People are often surprised by this. They do a session, feel lighter, and then have the most intense dreams they've had in years.

## Sleep is when the processing happens

Research from UC Berkeley found that REM sleep reduces the brain's emotional reaction to experiences from the day before. People who slept rated emotional images as less intense the next day, and their amygdala, the brain's alarm center, reacted less.

So when you stir up something emotional during the day, your brain keeps working on it at night. Vivid dreams can be a sign of that work in progress.

## What these dreams often look like

After emotional work, people commonly notice:

- Dreams set in old places, like a childhood home or a former school
- People from the past showing up
- The old fear playing out, but ending differently
- Waking up emotional without knowing why
- Dreams that feel unusually real or easy to remember

## The dream often matches what you worked on

This is the part I find so meaningful. After a Release Core session, the dreams that follow often reflect the exact pattern we worked on.

After one woman worked on the rule *I have to handle everything myself*, she dreamed two nights in a row that someone stepped in to help her. In one, a friend handed her the answers she'd been scrambling to find. In the other, someone stayed calm and took care of a problem she couldn't fix. Her nervous system was trying out the new rule.

## Not every dream after a session is pleasant

Sometimes the dream shows the old fear first. That doesn't mean the session didn't work. It often means your body is showing you what it's afraid will happen if it lets go. That's useful information, because it points to the next piece to rewire.

If you're having frequent nightmares, waking in a panic, or not sleeping well, talk with a doctor or mental health professional.

## Common questions

**Is it normal to have weird dreams after therapy?** Yes. Many people notice more vivid or emotional dreams after therapy, bodywork, breathwork, or other emotional work.

**How long do the vivid dreams last?** It varies. For many people they're strongest in the days right after, then settle as the pattern settles.

**Why do I wake up crying?** Emotions processed in a dream can carry into waking. Sometimes it's release, even relief. See [why do I cry for no reason when I'm healing?](/articles/why-do-i-cry-when-healing)

## Why it keeps coming back

If the same kind of dream keeps returning, the rule behind it probably hasn't fully changed. And most of the time, we can't tell which rule a dream is pointing to just by looking at it.
