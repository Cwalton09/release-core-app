The money came in. The relationship is good. You finally got what you were manifesting. And instead of joy, you feel... uneasy. Like something bad is about to happen to balance it out.

If you've ever thought "this is too good to last," you're not being negative. Your body may have learned that happiness isn't safe.

## What the research found

Psychologist Mohsen Joshanloo and an international team studied "fear of happiness" in more than 2,300 university students across 14 countries. Their 2014 study in the *Journal of Cross-Cultural Psychology* measured how much people agreed with beliefs like:

- "I prefer not to be too joyful, because usually joy is followed by sadness"
- "Disasters often follow good fortune"
- "Having lots of joy and fun causes bad things to happen"

Fear of happiness showed up in **every country they studied**. Later research found it was stronger in people who were lonelier and more perfectionistic, and a 2022 multi-country study found it was more common in people who reported less happy childhoods.

## Why happiness can feel like a threat

If good things in your past were often followed by something painful, like a fight after a happy day, a loss after a win, or punishment for being "too much," your nervous system learned a rule: *when it's good, get ready.*

So now when life goes well, your body doesn't relax. It braces.

This is one of the biggest hidden reasons manifesting stalls. You can want something with your whole heart, but if your body believes getting it is dangerous, it will quietly keep it away.

## Signs your body doesn't trust good things

- You feel uneasy when life is going well
- You downplay good news so you don't "jinx it"
- You wait for the other shoe to drop
- You pick fights or pull away when a relationship gets close
- You feel safer with a little struggle than with ease
- Your manifestations show up... then fall apart
