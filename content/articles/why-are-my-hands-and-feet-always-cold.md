Why are my hands and feet always cold? Your nervous system controls how much blood flows to your hands and feet. When you're cold or stressed, the fight-or-flight side tightens the small blood vessels in your fingers and toes to keep blood near your core and big muscles. For people whose nervous system is often on alert, that can mean cold hands and feet much of the time. Low iron, thyroid problems, and Raynaud's can also cause it.

You might notice cold, clammy hands before a hard conversation, icy toes in bed, or fingers that turn white or blue in the cold.

## See a doctor if…

Ongoing cold hands and feet can come from anemia, low thyroid, low blood pressure, circulation problems, diabetes, or medications like beta blockers. See a doctor if your fingers or toes turn white or blue, if cold hands come with numbness, sores, or pain, or if only one hand or foot is affected. Raynaud's that starts later in life can be linked to autoimmune conditions and should be checked.

## How your nervous system controls blood flow

The blood vessels in your fingers and toes have many sympathetic nerves. In fight-or-flight, those nerves tighten the vessels, sending blood away from your hands and feet. That's why your hands feel cold and clammy when you're nervous.

When your body feels safe, the vessels relax and warm blood returns. If your nervous system stays on alert, your hands and feet may rarely get that signal.

## What is Raynaud's?

In Raynaud's phenomenon, the small arteries in the fingers and toes clamp down in attacks, turning skin white, then blue, then red as blood returns. A 2026 scientific statement from the American Heart Association notes that attacks of primary Raynaud's are often triggered by cold or emotional stress. Primary Raynaud's usually starts in young women and is usually not dangerous. Secondary Raynaud's is linked to another condition and needs medical care.

## Signs stress may be part of it

- Your hands get cold and clammy when you're nervous
- Cold hands came with a stressful season
- You also have other stress signs, like a tight chest or racing heart
- Your hands warm up when you're relaxed or calm

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause cold hands?** Yes. Fight-or-flight tightens blood vessels in your hands, which makes them cold and sometimes sweaty.

**Why are my feet cold in bed?** Cold feet at night can come from circulation, a cool room, or a body that hasn't switched into rest yet. Warm socks and a slow wind-down can help.

**Is Raynaud's caused by stress?** Stress can trigger attacks, along with cold. It doesn't cause the condition itself.
