Can anxiety cause restless legs? Anxiety isn't a proven cause of restless legs syndrome (RLS), but the two are closely linked. A 2024 review found that people with RLS have much higher anxiety and depression than people without it. Stress and poor sleep can make restless legs worse, and restless legs make it harder to sleep, which feeds anxiety. Low iron, pregnancy, some medications, and kidney problems are also common causes.

You might feel an itchy, crawling, or buzzing urge to move your legs when you lie down, and moving is the only thing that helps.

## See a doctor if…

Restless legs can be a sign of low iron, even when you aren't anemic, so ask your doctor to check your ferritin. It can also come from pregnancy, kidney disease, nerve problems, or medications like some antidepressants and antihistamines. See a doctor if restless legs affect your sleep regularly. Leg pain with swelling, redness, or warmth needs care the same day.

## What research shows

A 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis in *Frontiers in Neurology* combined 21 studies. People with RLS had significantly more anxiety and depression symptoms than people without RLS.

The research shows a link, and it likely goes both ways. Poor sleep from restless legs can raise anxiety, and anxiety and stress can make the restless feeling stronger.

## How your nervous system plays a part

When your nervous system is on alert, your body is primed to move. At night, when you finally lie still, that unused energy can show up as restlessness, twitching, or an urge to get up. Stress also disrupts sleep, and tiredness can make restless legs worse.

## Signs stress may be part of it

- Your restless legs are worse during stressful weeks
- You feel wired and tired at night
- Your mind races when you lie down
- You also notice twitching or an inner buzzing

## Common questions

**Why are my legs restless at night?** Restless legs usually get worse in the evening and when you're still. Low iron, stress, caffeine, and some medications can make it worse.

**Can stress make restless legs worse?** Many people notice that. Stress affects sleep and keeps the nervous system on alert.

**What helps restless legs fast?** Walking, stretching, massage, and a warm bath help many people. Ask your doctor about checking your iron.
