Can stress cause a lupus flare? Many people with lupus say stress is one of their biggest flare triggers, and research supports a link between stress and autoimmune disease. A large study following more than 54,000 women for 24 years found that women with probable PTSD had nearly three times the risk of developing lupus, and any trauma exposure more than doubled it. Stress affects inflammation and immune activity, which is how the nervous system and lupus are connected.

You get through a hard month, and then your joints ache, the rash comes back, and the fatigue hits like a wall.

## Lupus needs medical care

Lupus is a serious autoimmune disease. Please keep working with your rheumatologist and never stop or change medication on your own. Get care right away for chest pain, trouble breathing, swelling in your legs, signs of kidney problems, or a high fever.

## How stress talks to your immune system

Your nervous system and immune system are in constant conversation. When you're under long-term stress, your body keeps stress hormones elevated and can increase inflammatory signals. Over time, the system that's supposed to keep inflammation in balance can stop working as well.

In autoimmune diseases like lupus, the immune system is already overactive. Extra inflammation from ongoing stress can add fuel. You can read more in [why autoimmune diseases flare with stress](/articles/why-autoimmune-flares-with-stress).

## What research shows

- In the Nurses' Health Study II, which followed 54,763 women for 24 years, trauma exposure and probable PTSD were linked to a higher risk of developing lupus. Probable PTSD was linked to nearly three times the risk.
- The lead researcher noted that trauma was a stronger predictor of lupus than smoking.
- People with lupus often report stress as a flare trigger, though more research is needed on exactly how much stress affects flares.

## Signs stress may be affecting your lupus

- Flares follow stressful events or seasons
- You feel run down, wired, or exhausted before a flare
- Sleep gets worse before flares
- You rarely get to fully rest

## Common questions

**Can emotional stress cause a lupus flare?** Many people with lupus report it, and research links stress and trauma to lupus and inflammation.

**Does stress cause lupus?** Lupus has many causes, including genetics, hormones, and environment. Research suggests trauma and stress may raise the risk in some people.

**How long after stress does a lupus flare happen?** It varies. Some people notice flares during stress, others shortly after.
