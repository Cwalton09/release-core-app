Your nervous system runs almost everything in your body: your heartbeat, your breathing, your digestion, your sleep, and how you react when something feels off. So when it's overloaded, it doesn't just show up as stress. It shows up everywhere.

Here are some of the most common signs. Count how many sound like you.

## In your body

- You wake up tired, no matter how much you sleep
- Your jaw, shoulders, or stomach are always tight
- Your stomach reacts to stress before you notice a thought
- You get headaches during or right after stressful weeks
- You can't take a full, satisfying breath
- You get sick right when things finally slow down

## In your mind

- Your thoughts race at night
- You read the same sentence three times
- You replay conversations long after they're over
- You expect the worst, even when things are fine
- You feel foggy or far away when things get to be too much

## In your emotions

- Small things set you off
- You feel numb when you think you should feel something
- You feel anxious "for no reason"
- Rest makes you more anxious, not less
- You feel guilty when you're not being productive

## In your relationships and life

- You keep the peace even when it costs you
- You notice every sound and read every room
- You keep attracting the same kind of relationship
- You get close to what you want, then pull back
- You tell the same painful story again and again, and it never feels lighter

## What your score means

This isn't a diagnosis, and any new or ongoing physical symptom deserves a visit to your doctor. But if you checked a lot of these, your nervous system may have been working overtime for a long time.

These aren't separate problems. They often come from the same place: a body that learned, at some point, that it wasn't safe to fully relax.
