You try to take a deep breath, and it doesn't feel satisfying. You yawn or sigh over and over, trying to get enough air. It feels like your lungs won't fill all the way.

That feeling is sometimes called "air hunger," and it's very common with anxiety and stress.

## Get your breathing checked first

Shortness of breath can come from asthma, heart and lung conditions, anemia, and other medical problems. If you suddenly can't breathe, have chest pain, or your lips turn blue, call 911. Talk to your doctor about ongoing breathing trouble.

## What dysfunctional breathing is

When there's no medical problem explaining it, researchers call this dysfunctional breathing. A 2016 review by Boulding and colleagues in the European Respiratory Review describes it as long-term changes in breathing pattern that cause breathlessness and other symptoms, without a disease that explains them.

One well-known type is hyperventilation, breathing faster or deeper than your body needs. Reviews note it is very often linked to anxiety, and that air hunger, dizziness, and palpitations can all come with it.

## How your nervous system changes your breathing

When your body senses danger, your breathing gets faster and moves up into your chest. Your shoulders and neck help you breathe instead of your diaphragm. That's useful in an emergency.

But if your nervous system stays on alert, that chest breathing can become your normal. And shallow chest breathing can leave you feeling like you never quite get a full breath.

## Signs your breathing is stress related

- You sigh or yawn a lot
- You feel like you can't get a satisfying breath
- Your breathing is mostly in your chest, not your belly
- It gets worse when you're anxious or focus on it
- It comes with tingling, dizziness, or a racing heart
- Your doctor hasn't found a medical cause

## Holding your breath to stay safe

Many people learned to hold their breath when they were scared or bracing, especially as kids. That pattern can stay long after the reason is gone. You can also read about [why your chest feels tight](/articles/why-does-my-chest-feel-tight-anxiety).
