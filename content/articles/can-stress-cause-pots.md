Can stress cause POTS? Stress alone isn't known to cause POTS. POTS often starts after a big hit to the body, like a viral illness, surgery, pregnancy, or a concussion. But stress can make POTS symptoms flare. POTS is a problem with the autonomic nervous system, the part that controls heart rate and blood pressure, and stress acts on that same system.

Many people with POTS notice their heart racing more, more dizziness, and worse fatigue during stressful times, even when their salt, fluids, and routine haven't changed.

## Work with your doctor

POTS is a real medical condition, not anxiety, and it should be diagnosed and treated by a doctor. Get urgent care if you faint and hurt yourself, have chest pain, or have a heart rate that stays very high while you're lying down. Working with your nervous system goes alongside your POTS care, not instead of it.

## What research shows about POTS triggers

An NIH expert summary on POTS reports that 20–50% of patients had a suspected viral infection before their symptoms began. Other reported triggers include concussion, surgery, pregnancy, and puberty. A review also notes that many patients say their symptoms started right after an acute stressor like these, while in others symptoms came on slowly.

Emotional stress isn't listed as a proven cause. But in many people, especially those with the hyperadrenergic type of POTS, symptoms get worse with physical and emotional stress.

## Why stress can make POTS worse

When you're stressed, your body releases adrenaline, which raises your heart rate. In POTS, your heart rate already jumps when you stand. Adding stress on top can push symptoms higher.

Stress also affects sleep, digestion, and how much you eat and drink, and all of these affect POTS. Living with an unpredictable illness is stressful in itself, and that can keep the body on alert.

## Signs stress may be part of your flares

- Your symptoms are worse during hard weeks
- Your heart races more after conflict or bad news
- You feel on guard about your body most of the time
- POTS started after an illness or event that was also frightening

## Common questions

**Is POTS just anxiety?** No. POTS is measured by real heart rate changes when you stand. Anxiety and POTS can happen together, and stress can worsen POTS, but POTS isn't "all in your head."

**Can a traumatic event trigger POTS?** Physical trauma like concussion is a known trigger. Emotional trauma hasn't been proven to cause POTS, though it can keep your nervous system on alert.

**Does calming my nervous system help POTS?** Many people find their symptoms are easier on calmer days. It's one part of care, along with your doctor's plan.
