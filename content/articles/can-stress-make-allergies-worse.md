Can stress make allergies worse? Research suggests it can. In one study, people under more ongoing emotional stress had more frequent allergy flares. Stress doesn't create allergies, but it changes how your immune system reacts to pollen, dust, and other triggers. When your nervous system is on alert, your body can overreact.

You might notice a stuffy nose, sneezing, itchy eyes, or a scratchy throat get worse during hard weeks, or flares that don't match the pollen count.

## See a doctor if…

Call 911 for trouble breathing, swelling of your lips, tongue, or throat, or hives with dizziness, since these can be signs of a serious allergic reaction. Please see a doctor if allergy symptoms are severe, last all year, or come with wheezing, sinus pain, or fever. Keep taking any allergy medications as directed.

## What research shows

A study in the *Annals of Allergy, Asthma and Immunology* followed 179 adults with daily online diaries over two 14-day periods. About 39% reported allergy symptoms. That group had higher stress scores, and higher perceived stress was linked to more allergy flares in both periods. People with more flares also had worse moods. The authors concluded that people with persistent emotional stress have more frequent allergy flares.

## Why stress makes allergies worse

- **Stress changes immune signals.** It can tip your immune system toward stronger allergic reactions.
- **Your body is on alert.** A system preparing for danger can overreact to harmless things.
- **Poor sleep.** Allergies disrupt sleep, and poor sleep adds stress.
- **The loop.** Flares make you feel worse, which adds more stress.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause allergy symptoms?** Anxiety can cause a tight throat or chest, and stress can make real allergies flare. A doctor can help tell the difference.

**Why are my allergies worse when I'm stressed?** Stress changes how your immune system reacts, which can make flares more frequent.

**Can calming down help my allergies?** It can support your allergy treatment, but it doesn't replace it.
