Can stress cause heartburn? Yes, stress can trigger or worsen heartburn in many people. Research suggests stress doesn't always mean more acid. Instead, it can make your esophagus more sensitive, so the same amount of acid burns more. Stress also changes how you eat, sleep, and breathe, and all of these can add to reflux.

You might notice it during a hard week, after an argument, or lying in bed with your mind racing. The burning comes even when you ate the same foods as always.

## Get urgent care if…

Chest pain isn't always heartburn. Call 911 if you have chest pain or pressure with shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, or pain spreading to your arm, jaw, neck, or back. These can be signs of a heart attack. Also see a doctor if you have trouble swallowing, vomiting, black stools, weight loss, or heartburn more than twice a week.

## What research shows

In a 2008 study in *Gastroenterology*, people with heartburn had acid placed in their esophagus while they listened to stressful sounds. During stress, they noticed the acid sooner, rated the burning as stronger, and were more sensitive to it overall. The researchers linked this to how strongly people reacted to the stress.

An earlier study followed people with reflux during stressful periods. Many of them said life stress made their heartburn worse.

That's why heartburn can flare on hard days even when nothing about your food changed.

## How stress reaches your esophagus

When your nervous system is on alert, it turns up how strongly you feel signals from your body, including from your gut. Stress can also slow how quickly your stomach empties. You may eat faster, sleep less, drink more coffee, or hold tension in your belly and chest, and each of these can add to reflux.

## Signs stress may be part of your heartburn

- It flares on stressful days or after hard conversations
- It's worse at night when your mind finally slows down
- Food changes and antacids help only partly
- You also have a tight chest, a lump in your throat, or a nervous stomach

## Common questions

**Can anxiety feel like heartburn?** Yes. Anxiety can cause chest tightness, burning, and a lump-in-the-throat feeling. If you're not sure what's causing chest symptoms, get checked.

**What helps stress heartburn fast?** Sitting upright, slow breathing, and avoiding big meals before bed can help. Talk with your doctor about medicine if you need it.

**Can stress cause heartburn every day?** It can make heartburn more frequent. Daily heartburn should be checked by a doctor.
