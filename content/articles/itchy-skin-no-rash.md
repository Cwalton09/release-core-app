Why is your skin itchy with no rash? There are medical causes worth checking, like dry skin, thyroid problems, low iron, liver or kidney issues, and medication side effects. But stress and anxiety are also strongly linked to itching without a rash. Your skin is packed with nerve endings that talk directly to your nervous system, and when that system is on high alert, it can turn up itch signals even when nothing is wrong with the skin itself.

You itch, you scratch, you look, and there's nothing there. No bumps, no bites, no rash. And it's often worse at night.

## Get the medical causes checked

Please see a doctor if itching lasts more than a couple of weeks, keeps you up at night, or comes with weight loss, fatigue, yellowing skin, or night sweats. Ask about:

- Thyroid function
- Iron and ferritin
- Liver and kidney function
- Blood sugar
- Any new medications

## How stress turns up itch

Itch is a nerve signal. Your skin's nerve endings send it up to your spinal cord and brain, and your brain decides how loud it feels.

When your nervous system is stressed, a few things can happen:

- Stress hormones and nerve chemicals can make skin nerves more reactive
- Mast cells in the skin can release histamine (see [can stress cause hives?](/articles/can-stress-cause-hives))
- Your brain pays more attention to body sensations, so small itches feel bigger

Researchers describe an itch-anxiety cycle: chronic itch raises anxiety, and anxiety and stress make the itch worse. In one study, people with chronic itch scored higher on anxiety, depression, and health anxiety than people without it.

## Why it's worse at night

At night, there's nothing to distract you, and your attention turns inward. Your body's natural rhythms also shift at night in ways that can make skin more itchy. And if your nervous system revs up when you finally lie down, you notice every sensation.

## What about the crawling feeling?

Some people feel like bugs are crawling on their skin. This is called formication. It can come with anxiety, but it can also come from medications, withdrawal, nerve problems, and other medical causes, so please talk to your doctor about it.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause itching all over?** Yes. Anxiety and stress are linked to itching without a rash, and the itch can feed the anxiety back.

**What does stress itching feel like?** It often moves around the body, comes in waves, and gets worse during stress or at night.

**How do I know if my itching is stress?** If medical causes are ruled out and the itch rises and falls with your stress, your nervous system is likely part of it.
