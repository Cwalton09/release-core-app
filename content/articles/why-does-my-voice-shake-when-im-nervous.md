Why does my voice shake when I'm nervous? Your voice is made by small muscles in your throat, and stress changes how they work. When fight-or-flight kicks in, adrenaline makes muscles tense and tremble, your breathing gets shallow, and your throat tightens. Together, that can make your voice shake, crack, go higher, or feel like it's stuck. Research shows stress tends to raise the pitch of the voice.

You might notice it when you present at work, speak up in a group, say something emotional, or confront someone.

## See a doctor if…

A shaky voice that happens all the time, not just when nervous, can be a voice tremor or another condition. See a doctor or voice specialist if your voice is hoarse for more than a few weeks, if you have trouble swallowing, or if the shake comes with tremor in your hands or head. Sudden voice loss with weakness or trouble speaking needs emergency care.

## How stress changes your voice

Speaking needs steady breath and relaxed vocal cords. In fight-or-flight:

- Breathing gets fast and shallow, so there's less steady air behind your voice
- Throat and neck muscles tighten, raising your pitch
- Adrenaline makes muscles tremble, including the small ones in your larynx
- Your mouth may go dry, making your voice catch

## What research shows

A 2025 review and meta-analysis in *Stress and Health* looked at ten studies on stress and the voice. It found that stress tended to raise the voice's pitch, especially in women, likely through tension in the larynx muscles. The authors noted the research is still early, the effect may be smaller than it looks, and more studies are needed.

## Signs nerves are behind it

- It only happens when you're nervous or emotional
- It's worse when you think about it
- Your hands shake or your heart races too
- It settles once you get going or relax

## Common questions

**How do I stop my voice from shaking?** Slow down, breathe low into your belly before you speak, let your exhale be long, and speak a little lower and slower.

**Why does my voice shake when I cry or get emotional?** Strong emotion tightens the throat and changes your breathing, which affects your voice.

**Can people hear my voice shake?** Usually much less than you think. It feels bigger from the inside.

## Why it keeps coming back
