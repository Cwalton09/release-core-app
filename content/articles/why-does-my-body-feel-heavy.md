Why does my body feel heavy? A heavy, weighed-down body is often a sign of exhaustion, low mood, illness, or a nervous system in shutdown. After long stress, your body can shift into a protective low-energy state that makes your limbs feel like lead and every task feel huge. Inflammation from illness or long-term stress can create the same heavy, slowed-down feeling. Many medical conditions can cause it too, so it's worth getting checked.

You might wake up already exhausted, feel like you're moving through water, or struggle to get off the couch even when you want to.

## See a doctor if…

A heavy body can come from anemia, thyroid problems, low vitamin D or B12, sleep apnea, infections, diabetes, depression, medications, and more. Please see a doctor if it lasts more than a couple of weeks or comes with weight changes, fever, shortness of breath, or swelling. Get emergency care for sudden heaviness or weakness on one side, trouble speaking, or chest pain. If you feel hopeless or think about harming yourself, call or text 988.

## How your nervous system can make you feel heavy

Your nervous system has more than fight-or-flight. When stress feels too big for too long, your body can move into shutdown, sometimes called freeze. In this state, energy, motivation, and movement all turn down to protect you. From the inside, it feels like heaviness, fog, and wanting to lie down.

Depression is also known for a heavy, "leaden" feeling in the arms and legs.

## What research shows about inflammation

When you're sick, your immune system releases chemicals called cytokines. A 2008 review in *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* describes how these signals reach the brain and cause "sickness behavior": fatigue, heaviness, low motivation, and wanting to withdraw. Long-term stress can also raise inflammation, which may be one reason stressed bodies feel heavy and drained.

## Signs it may be your nervous system

- It started during or after a long, hard season
- Your tests came back normal
- You feel flat or numb, not just tired
- Rest doesn't refresh you
- It eases a little when you feel safe, connected, or excited

## Common questions

**Why does my body feel heavy and tired?** Common causes include poor sleep, low iron, thyroid problems, depression, and long-term stress. A doctor can help rule out medical causes.

**Can anxiety make your body feel heavy?** Yes. After long periods of anxiety, the body can crash into shutdown, which feels heavy and drained.

**Why do my legs feel heavy?** Heavy legs can come from circulation problems, low iron, or exhaustion, and should be checked, especially with swelling or pain.
