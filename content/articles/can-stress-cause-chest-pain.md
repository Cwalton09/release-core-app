Can stress cause chest pain? Yes. Stress and anxiety can tighten the muscles in your chest, speed up your heart, change your breathing, and make you more aware of every sensation, all of which can cause real chest pain or pressure. But chest pain can also be a heart problem, so it should always be taken seriously and checked, especially if it's new.

It's one of the most frightening symptoms there is. Your chest hurts, your mind goes straight to your heart, and the fear makes everything worse.

## Call 911 if…

Call 911 right away if chest pain is crushing, squeezing, or heavy; spreads to your arm, jaw, neck, or back; or comes with shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, or lightheadedness. Don't wait to see if it passes. It's always better to be checked and told it's anxiety than to miss a heart attack.

## How stress causes chest pain

When your nervous system senses danger, it gets your body ready to fight or run:

- **Muscles tighten**, including the muscles between your ribs and across your chest
- **Your heart speeds up** and may pound or flutter
- **Breathing gets fast and shallow**, which can cause chest tightness and tingling
- **Your stomach can react**, and acid reflux can feel like chest pain
- **You become hyperaware** of sensations in your chest, which can make them feel bigger

If your nervous system stays on alert for a long time, those muscles can stay tight, and the chest pain can keep coming back.

## What anxiety chest pain often feels like

- Sharp, stabbing, or aching pain that comes and goes
- Tightness or pressure that eases as you calm down
- Pain that shows up with stress, worry, or panic
- Often comes with a racing heart, tingling, or shaky breath

Even if your chest pain fits this pattern, please get it checked at least once, and again if it changes.

## Common questions

**How do I know if chest pain is anxiety or my heart?** You can't always tell on your own. That's why new or severe chest pain needs medical care.

**Can stress cause chest pain for days?** Yes. Tense chest muscles can stay sore, and ongoing stress can keep the pain coming back.

**Can stress cause sharp chest pain?** Yes. Muscle tension and fast breathing can cause sharp, stabbing pains.

## Why it keeps coming back
