Can stress cause a heart attack or stroke? Long-term stress is linked to a higher risk of both. In the large INTERHEART study of people in 52 countries, stress was one of the risk factors tied to heart attacks. Stress raises blood pressure and heart rate, increases inflammation, and affects sleep and habits. A sudden shock can also cause a temporary heart condition called broken heart syndrome. Stress usually works alongside other risk factors, not alone.

If you've had a scare, or you've watched someone you love go through one, it makes sense to wonder how much stress had to do with it.

## Know the warning signs

Call 911 right away for:

- **Heart attack:** chest pain or pressure, pain spreading to the arm, jaw, neck, or back, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea, or lightheadedness. Women often have subtler signs, like unusual fatigue, nausea, or back or jaw pain.
- **Stroke:** use **BE FAST**: Balance loss, Eyes (vision changes), Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech trouble, Time to call 911.

## What the research shows

The INTERHEART study compared more than 11,000 people who'd had a heart attack with more than 13,000 who hadn't. People who'd had a heart attack were more likely to report stress at work or home, stressful life events, and depression. Stressful life events in the past year were linked to about 1.5 times the odds of a heart attack.

Stress also shows up in stroke research as a risk factor, alongside high blood pressure, smoking, and diabetes.

## How stress affects your heart

When your nervous system stays in fight or flight:

- Your heart rate and [blood pressure stay higher](/articles/can-stress-cause-high-blood-pressure)
- Inflammation increases
- Blood can become more likely to clot
- Sleep, eating, drinking, and exercise often suffer

And a sudden emotional shock can cause [broken heart syndrome](/articles/can-you-die-from-a-broken-heart), when a surge of stress hormones temporarily weakens the heart muscle.

## What this means for you

Stress is one piece of your heart health, not the whole story. Please keep up with your doctor, know your blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar numbers, and take any medication as prescribed. Calming your nervous system is something you can add to that care.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause a heart attack?** Anxiety on its own doesn't usually cause a heart attack, but long-term stress and anxiety are linked to higher heart risk. Anxiety symptoms can also feel like a heart attack, so get checked if you're unsure.

**Can stress cause a stroke?** Long-term stress is considered a risk factor for stroke, mostly through effects like high blood pressure.

**Can a sudden shock cause a heart attack?** A sudden shock can trigger broken heart syndrome, and in people with heart disease, intense stress can trigger a heart attack.
