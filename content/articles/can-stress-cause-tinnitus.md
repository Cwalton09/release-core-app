Can stress cause ringing in your ears? Stress is strongly linked to tinnitus, the ringing, buzzing, or humming sound only you can hear. Tinnitus often starts with hearing changes or noise exposure, but stress can make it start, get louder, or feel harder to ignore. In one study, emotional exhaustion was the strongest predictor of having tinnitus and how severe it was, even after accounting for hearing loss.

It's quiet in the room, and you hear a high-pitched ring. On stressful days it's louder. At night it's all you can hear.

## See a doctor

Please see a doctor or audiologist if tinnitus is new, only in one ear, pulses with your heartbeat, or comes with hearing loss, dizziness, or ear pain. Sudden hearing loss is an emergency, so get seen right away.

## How stress affects tinnitus

Tinnitus is often a sign that the hearing system has changed, from noise, age, ear problems, or other causes. Your brain then fills in the missing signal with sound.

Your nervous system decides how much attention that sound gets. When you're stressed, your brain is on alert, scanning for anything unusual. Your stress response and the brain areas that process sound and emotion work closely together, so stress can turn tinnitus up and make it feel more distressing.

Then a loop can start: the ringing causes stress, and stress makes the ringing louder.

## What research shows

- A study published in *Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics* found emotional exhaustion was the strongest predictor of tinnitus and how severe it was, even after controlling for hearing loss.
- Reviews of the research describe stress as both a possible trigger and something that makes tinnitus worse, though researchers say more study is needed.

## Signs stress is part of your tinnitus

- It gets louder on stressful days
- It started or worsened during a hard season
- It's hardest to ignore at night or when you're anxious
- You also clench your jaw or tense your neck and shoulders (see [why do I clench my jaw?](/articles/why-do-i-clench-my-jaw))
- It eases a little when you feel calm

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause ringing in the ears?** Anxiety and stress are strongly linked to tinnitus and can make it louder or more noticeable.

**Can stress tinnitus go away?** For some people, it fades as stress settles. For others, it becomes less noticeable as the nervous system calms.

**Why is my tinnitus louder at night?** It's quieter around you, and your brain has fewer sounds to focus on.
