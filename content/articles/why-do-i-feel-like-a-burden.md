Why do you feel like a burden? Usually because your nervous system learned that having needs wasn't safe. Maybe your feelings were too much for the people around you, maybe asking for help led to disappointment, or maybe you learned to make yourself small to keep the peace. Now, any time you need something, your body braces, and you feel like you're asking for too much.

You hold back from texting a friend when you're struggling. You apologize for talking about your feelings. You'd rather do everything yourself than feel like you're bothering someone.

## If you're in pain right now

If feeling like a burden ever turns into thinking people would be better off without you, please reach out right now. Call or text 988 in the U.S., or contact a crisis line where you are. You matter, and that feeling is not the truth.

## Where the burden feeling comes from

Your nervous system learns from how people responded to your needs, especially when you were young. If your needs were met with stress, frustration, silence, or punishment, your body may have concluded:

- *My needs are too much*
- *If I need something, I'll be rejected*
- *I have to take care of myself so I don't bother anyone*
- *I'm only okay if I'm not causing problems*

Those rules kept you safe back then. Now they keep you alone.

## How it shows up

- Apologizing for needing anything
- Downplaying your pain
- Not asking for help, even when you really need it
- Feeling guilty when someone does something for you
- Being the helper for everyone else
- Shutting down instead of sharing

## Common questions

**Why do I feel like a burden when I talk about my feelings?** Your nervous system may have learned that sharing feelings led to rejection or stress for others.

**Why do I feel like a burden to my partner?** Old rules about needs often show up most in close relationships.

**How do I stop feeling like a burden?** Understanding where it came from helps, but the feeling usually changes when your nervous system updates the rule underneath.

## Why it keeps coming back

When you keep feeling like too much, your nervous system is still following a rule about needs that it learned long ago.

The real root is often very different from what you think. It usually isn't about the people in your life now. It's an old rule your body learned long before, like *my needs push people away* or *I have to handle everything alone*, and today's moments are just turning up its volume.
