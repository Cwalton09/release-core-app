Can grief cause physical symptoms? Yes. Grief isn't only emotional. It's one of the biggest stressors a nervous system can face, and it shows up in the body as exhaustion, aches, chest tightness, headaches, stomach problems, poor sleep, and getting sick more often. Research shows bereavement is linked to more physical symptoms and illness, especially in the first weeks and months, and to higher inflammation in the body.

You might feel like you've been hit by a truck, have a heavy ache in your chest, lose your appetite, or catch every cold going around after someone you love dies.

## Please take care of yourself

Grief is not an illness, and most people move through it without special treatment. But please see a doctor if you have chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, or symptoms that are new or worsening. Call 911 for chest pain or pressure. If grief feels unbearable or you think about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US. Grief support groups and counselors can help.

## What research shows

**Bereavement and health.** A review in *The Lancet* found that bereavement is linked to a higher risk of death, especially in the early weeks and months after the loss, along with more physical symptoms, more illness, and more doctor visits.

**Grief and inflammation.** In a 2019 study of people who had recently lost a spouse, those with stronger grief had higher levels of inflammation in the body. Inflammation is linked to fatigue, aches, low mood, and heart problems.

The heart can be affected too. See [can you die from a broken heart](/articles/can-you-die-from-a-broken-heart).

## How grief reaches your body

When you lose someone, your nervous system goes into a kind of alarm. Stress hormones rise, sleep breaks down, appetite changes, and your immune system shifts. Your body is trying to survive a loss that feels unsurvivable.

## Common physical signs of grief

- Exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix
- A heavy, tight, or hollow feeling in your chest
- Aches and pains all over
- Headaches
- Nausea, no appetite, or stomach upset
- Getting sick more often
- Trouble sleeping or sleeping too much
- Feeling foggy and forgetful. See [grief brain fog](/articles/grief-brain-fog).

## Common questions

**How long do physical symptoms of grief last?** Often they ease over weeks to months. Grief comes in waves, so symptoms can return around anniversaries and reminders.

**Can grief make you sick?** It can make you more likely to get sick, through stress on your immune system, sleep, and self-care.

**Why does my chest hurt when I'm grieving?** Grief often brings chest tightness from stress and tense muscles. Always get chest pain checked, because grief can also strain the heart.
