Why can't you concentrate? Often because your nervous system is under stress. The part of your brain that handles focus, planning, and working memory, the prefrontal cortex, is the most sensitive to stress. Research shows even mild stress can quickly weaken it. When your body feels unsafe, it shifts energy toward survival and away from careful thinking, so focus slips.

You read the same paragraph five times. You walk into a room and forget why. Your to-do list sits there, and your mind won't land on anything.

## What research shows

In a 2009 review, neuroscientist Amy Arnsten explained that the prefrontal cortex is the brain region most sensitive to stress. Even mild, uncontrollable stress can cause a rapid loss of prefrontal abilities like focus and working memory. Longer-term stress can change the structure of this part of the brain.

In other words, when you're stressed, your brain is designed to stop doing careful work and start scanning for danger.

## Why stress steals your focus

- **Survival comes first**: your brain shifts to fast, reactive thinking
- **Scanning**: part of your attention is always watching for threats
- **Worry loops** take up the space you need to focus
- **Poor sleep** weakens attention and memory
- **Exhaustion** from running on stress for a long time

## Is it ADHD?

Trouble concentrating can be part of ADHD, depression, anxiety, thyroid problems, sleep disorders, or medication side effects. If focus problems have been lifelong or are getting in the way of work, school, or relationships, please talk with a doctor. See [ADHD and the nervous system](/articles/adhd-and-the-nervous-system).

## Why trying harder doesn't help

Pushing yourself to focus often adds more stress, which shuts focus down even more. That's why concentration usually comes back when your nervous system settles, not when you force it.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety make it hard to concentrate?** Yes. Anxiety keeps your brain in threat mode, which weakens focus.

**Why can't I focus on anything lately?** Ongoing stress, poor sleep, or big life changes can all wear down your focus.

**Why can I focus on some things but not others?** Your brain finds it easier to focus on things that feel engaging or safe, and harder on things that feel stressful.

## Why it keeps coming back

If focus keeps slipping away, your nervous system is still spending its energy on staying safe.

The real root is often very different from what you think. It usually isn't the task, your phone, or a lack of discipline.
