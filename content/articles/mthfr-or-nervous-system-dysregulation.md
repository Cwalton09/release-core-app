Is it MTHFR or a dysregulated nervous system? The symptoms people blame on MTHFR, like anxiety, fatigue, brain fog, low moods, and stomach trouble, are the same symptoms a stressed, stuck nervous system creates. That's why the two get confused so often. Your doctor can check the blood markers. Your nervous system needs a different kind of question.

If you've spent hours in MTHFR groups trying to figure out the right supplement, you're not alone. And if nothing has quite worked, there may be a reason.

## The symptom lists are nearly identical

Here's what people commonly list as "MTHFR symptoms":

- Anxiety or feeling on edge
- Fatigue that sleep doesn't fix
- Brain fog
- Low moods
- Headaches
- Digestive trouble
- Feeling "off" with certain vitamins

And here's what a [dysregulated nervous system](/articles/dysregulated-nervous-system-symptoms) commonly causes:

- Anxiety or feeling on edge
- Fatigue, often with feeling wired at night
- Brain fog
- Low moods or feeling numb
- Tension headaches
- Digestive trouble
- Feeling sensitive to things that never used to bother you

It's almost the same list.

## What the research says about MTHFR

MTHFR variants are very common. The CDC says people with them can still process all types of folate, and their folate levels run only slightly lower.

In 2013, the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics recommended **against** routine MTHFR testing, saying it has minimal clinical usefulness. The link between MTHFR and mood is real but small, and the link with anxiety is unclear. You can read the full breakdown in [MTHFR, anxiety, and depression](/articles/mthfr-anxiety-depression).

That doesn't mean your symptoms aren't real. It means the gene may not be the main reason you have them.

## What your doctor can actually check

The gene result alone doesn't tell you much. What can tell you something is your actual levels. Ask your doctor about:

- **Homocysteine**, the marker most linked to MTHFR
- **Vitamin B12**
- **Folate**
- A **complete blood count**, to look for anemia

If those come back normal and you still feel anxious, exhausted, and foggy, that's a strong sign to look at your nervous system.

## Signs it's your nervous system

- Your symptoms rise and fall with stress
- You feel better on vacation, even on the same supplements
- You started feeling this way during a hard season, not as a child
- Certain people or places make it worse
- Your labs are normal, but you still don't feel like yourself
- You've tried several vitamin protocols and none of them stuck

## Why they get confused

A nervous system stuck in protection affects your whole body. It can slow digestion, change how you absorb nutrients, disrupt sleep, and even raise homocysteine during stress. See [can stress affect how your body absorbs nutrients?](/articles/stress-and-nutrient-absorption) and [can stress raise homocysteine?](/articles/can-stress-raise-homocysteine)

So you can end up with real changes in your body that look like a methylation problem, when the bigger driver is a nervous system that never gets to stand down.

Never stop or start medication or supplements without talking to your doctor.
