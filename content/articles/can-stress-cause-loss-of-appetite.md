Can stress cause loss of appetite? Yes. When your nervous system shifts into fight-or-flight, your body moves energy away from digestion, and stress hormones can switch off hunger for a while. Some people can't eat at all during hard times and lose weight without trying. Others eat more under stress. Both are common, and research shows stress can push appetite in either direction.

You might feel a knot in your stomach, feel full after a few bites, forget to eat, or feel sick at the thought of food.

## See a doctor if…

Unexplained weight loss always deserves a check, even if you're stressed. Please see a doctor if you've lost more than 5% of your weight in 6 to 12 months without trying, or if loss of appetite comes with fever, night sweats, vomiting, trouble swallowing, belly pain, or changes in your stool. Thyroid problems, digestive conditions, depression, medications, and other illnesses can also cause it. If you're restricting food on purpose or feel out of control around food, talk with a doctor or an eating disorder specialist.

## How stress turns off hunger

In a real emergency, eating isn't a priority. Your brain releases a stress hormone called CRH, which helps start the stress response and can suppress appetite. Adrenaline slows digestion, and your stomach may feel tight or queasy.

When stress goes on for a long time, cortisol stays higher, and that can push some people toward cravings and eating more instead. That's why the same stress can make one person stop eating and another reach for snacks.

## What research shows

A 2013 review in *Minerva Endocrinologica* looked at stress and eating. It found that stress affects both appetite and food choices, and that people respond differently. Some eat less and lose weight, especially during intense or sudden stress. Others eat more, especially high-sugar and high-fat foods, during long-term stress.

## Signs stress may be behind it

- Your appetite dropped during a hard time
- Your stomach feels tight, nervous, or queasy
- You forget to eat when you're busy or worried
- Your appetite comes back on calm days or vacation

## Common questions

**Can anxiety make you lose weight?** It can, by lowering appetite and speeding up your body. Talk with a doctor about unexplained weight loss.

**How long does stress loss of appetite last?** It often returns as stress eases. If it lasts more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor.

**What helps when I can't eat from stress?** Small, simple meals, smoothies, and eating on a schedule can help until your appetite returns.
