Why do I feel pressure in my head? One of the most common causes is a tension-type headache: a pressing or tight feeling, often like a band around your forehead, temples, or the back of your head. Stress is one of its biggest triggers. When your nervous system is on alert, you tighten the muscles in your scalp, jaw, neck, and shoulders, and your brain becomes more sensitive to pain. Sinus problems, poor sleep, eye strain, and dehydration can also cause head pressure.

You might feel it at the end of a stressful day, behind your eyes after hours at a screen, or as a dull squeeze that won't let up.

## Get emergency help if…

Call 911 or go to the ER for a sudden, severe headache (the worst of your life), head pressure with weakness, numbness, confusion, trouble speaking, vision loss, a stiff neck with fever, fainting, or a headache after a head injury. See a doctor for headaches that are new, getting worse, wake you up, or change with position or coughing.

## What research shows

A 2021 review in *Nature Reviews Disease Primers* calls tension-type headache the most common neurological disorder in the world. It describes it as a pressing or tightening pain on both sides of the head that doesn't get worse with routine activity. The review explains that pain likely starts in tight, tender muscles around the head and neck. When headaches become frequent, the nervous system itself can become more sensitive, so headaches come more easily.

## How stress creates head pressure

In fight-or-flight, your body braces. You clench your jaw, raise your shoulders, furrow your brow, and hold your neck stiff, often without noticing. Those muscles connect to your scalp. Over hours, the tension builds into pressure.

Stress also affects sleep and breathing, and shallow breathing and poor sleep can make headaches more likely.

## Signs stress may be behind it

- The pressure builds through stressful days
- It feels like a tight band, not a throbbing pain
- Your jaw, neck, or shoulders are tight too
- It eases with rest, a massage, or a calm day
- Your doctor has ruled out other causes

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause head pressure?** Yes. Anxiety keeps your head and neck muscles tense and makes you more sensitive to pain.

**Why do I feel pressure behind my eyes?** It can come from tension headaches, eye strain, or sinus congestion. If it comes with vision changes, see a doctor right away.

**How do I relieve stress head pressure?** Unclench your jaw, drop your shoulders, take breaks from screens, drink water, and rest. Talk with your doctor about pain relief if you need it often.
