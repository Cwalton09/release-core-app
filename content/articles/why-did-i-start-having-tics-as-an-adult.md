Most people think of tics as something kids have. So when you start blinking, jerking, or making sounds you can't control as an adult, it can be scary and confusing.

You might wonder what's wrong with you. You might feel embarrassed at work or around people who've known you for years.

Here's what can be going on.

## See a doctor first

New tics in adulthood are less common than in childhood, and they deserve a proper medical check. A doctor can look at possible causes, including medications, other health conditions, or a neurological issue, and help you understand what you're dealing with.

Please don't skip this step. Getting checked out also takes away some of the fear of not knowing.

## Some adults had tics as kids

Many adults with "new" tics actually had mild tics as a child that faded over time. Sometimes they come back during a stressful season of life, like a new job, a loss, a breakup, a move, or becoming a parent.

You might not have even remembered having them until they returned.

## Stress can bring them to the surface

Whether your tics are new or returning, stress is one of the most common things that makes them louder. When your nervous system has been on alert for a long time, it has less room to keep everything contained.

That's why tics often show up after a long stretch of pressure, even if the most stressful moment has already passed. Your body can take a while to catch up. You can read more about [tics and the nervous system](/articles/tics-and-the-nervous-system).

## Signs stress is part of your picture

- Your tics started or got worse during a hard season of life
- They flare when you're tired, worried, or overwhelmed
- They ease on vacation, on calm days, or when you're absorbed in something
- You've been feeling tense, wired, or on edge for a while
- You also notice anxiety, poor sleep, or trouble relaxing

## Why it keeps coming back

Most of the time, we don't know the real reason behind what's driving the trigger. We can guess, but the actual trigger is usually something we would never think of on our own. And until it's found, your body will keep holding the stress it's carrying.
