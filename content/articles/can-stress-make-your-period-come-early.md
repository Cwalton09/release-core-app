Can stress make your period come early? It can. Stress is linked to irregular cycles, which can mean a period that comes late, early, or with a different flow than usual. Your menstrual cycle is controlled by hormones that start in the brain, and the same brain areas respond to stress. When your nervous system is under pressure, the timing of your cycle can shift.

You might get your period a week early during a hard month, have two periods close together, or notice spotting, heavier, or lighter bleeding than usual.

## See a doctor if…

Please see a doctor if your cycles are often shorter than 21 days, if you bleed between periods or after sex, if bleeding is very heavy (soaking a pad or tampon every hour), or if you have severe pain. If there's any chance you could be pregnant, take a test, since bleeding in early pregnancy can look like an early period. Thyroid problems, PCOS, fibroids, and perimenopause can also change your cycle.

## What research shows

A 2024 systematic review in *Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews* looked at 41 studies of healthy adults. Most studies found a link between psychological stress and menstrual problems, with irregular periods and changes in flow being the most common. Stressors included the COVID-19 pandemic, school, and work. The authors describe stress as a modifiable risk factor for irregular menstruation.

## How stress shifts your cycle

Your brain's hypothalamus sends signals that control ovulation. Stress hormones can interfere with those signals, changing when you ovulate. That can make your cycle shorter or longer than usual. See [how stress affects your hormones](/articles/how-stress-affects-your-hormones).

## Signs stress is affecting your cycle

- Your cycle changes during stressful months
- It settles when life calms down
- You notice more PMS symptoms during stress
- Your period comes early or late after a big event

## Common questions

**How early can stress make your period come?** It varies. A few days to a week is common. Cycles shorter than 21 days should be checked.

**Can stress cause two periods in one month?** Stress can shorten a cycle enough that two periods fall in the same month. If it keeps happening, see a doctor.

**Will my cycle go back to normal?** Often, once stress eases. If it doesn't settle after a few months, talk to a doctor.
