Can stress cause bloating? Yes, stress can trigger or worsen bloating and gas in many people. Your gut and brain talk constantly through the vagus nerve and stress hormones. When your nervous system is on alert, digestion slows down, your gut can become more sensitive, and normal amounts of gas can feel tight, full, and uncomfortable.

You might notice it on workdays but not on vacation, before a hard conversation, or during a stressful season. You eat the same foods, but your stomach swells and you feel like you're holding a balloon.

## See a doctor if…

Bloating can have many causes, including food intolerances, celiac disease, IBS, constipation, and other conditions. Please see a doctor if bloating is new and won't go away, or if it comes with weight loss, blood in your stool, vomiting, fever, trouble swallowing, or severe pain. Bloating that lasts for weeks in women over 50 should always be checked.

## How stress reaches your gut

Your gut has its own nervous system, sometimes called the "second brain," and it's wired to your brain. In fight-or-flight mode, your body moves blood and energy away from digestion. Food and gas can move more slowly, which can leave you feeling full and swollen.

Stress can also make the nerves in your gut more sensitive. Researchers call this visceral hypersensitivity. It means normal stretching from food or gas feels bigger and more uncomfortable than it is. Many people also breathe shallowly or swallow more air when they're tense, which adds to it.

## What research shows

A 2023 review in *The Journal of Physiology* looked at how stress affects the gut. It found that both short-term and long-term stress can change gut movement, gut sensitivity, the gut lining, and gut bacteria. These are the same systems involved in bloating.

Research on IBS, where bloating is one of the most common symptoms, describes it as a disorder of the gut-brain connection, and stress is a well-known trigger for flares.

## Signs stress may be part of your bloating

- It's worse on stressful days and better on calm days or vacation
- It started during or after a hard season
- Food changes and tests haven't fully explained it
- It comes with other stress signs, like a tight chest, poor sleep, or a racing mind
- Your stomach feels "nervous" or tight before you even eat

## Common questions

**Can stress make you gassy?** Yes. Stress can change how quickly food moves through your gut and how much air you swallow, and both can mean more gas.

**How long does stress bloating last?** It often eases within hours to a day once you calm down. If it keeps coming back for weeks, talk with your doctor.

**Why am I bloated even when I eat healthy?** Healthy food helps, but a stressed nervous system can still slow digestion and make your gut more sensitive, no matter what's on your plate.
