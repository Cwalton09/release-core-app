Can anxiety cause vertigo? Anxiety can cause dizziness, unsteadiness, and a feeling of being off balance. It can also keep dizziness going long after an inner ear problem has healed. Doctors now recognize a condition called persistent postural-perceptual dizziness (PPPD), where daily unsteadiness lasts for months. It's often triggered by a vestibular problem, an illness, or psychological distress, and it's linked to how the brain processes balance and threat.

You might feel like you're rocking or swaying, feel worse in grocery stores or busy places, feel unsteady when standing or walking, or feel dizzy watching moving things.

## See a doctor if…

Call 911 for sudden dizziness with a severe headache, weakness, numbness, trouble speaking, vision loss, or trouble walking, since these can be signs of a stroke. Please see a doctor for new dizziness, spinning vertigo, hearing loss, ringing in one ear, or fainting. Inner ear problems, blood pressure changes, medications, and other conditions can cause dizziness and are often treatable.

## What research shows

In 2017, the Bárány Society, an international group of balance experts, published diagnostic criteria for PPPD. It describes dizziness, unsteadiness, or non-spinning vertigo on most days for three months or more. It gets worse with standing, moving, and busy or moving visual scenes.

The experts noted that PPPD can be triggered by inner ear problems, other medical illnesses, or psychological distress. Research suggests it may come from changes in how the brain controls posture, processes senses, and judges threat. They classify it as a real functional disorder, not a structural or psychiatric one.

## How your nervous system affects balance

- **Your brain combines signals.** Balance comes from your inner ear, eyes, and body working together.
- **Alert mode changes the mix.** When you're on guard, your brain relies more on vision and stiffens your posture.
- **Bracing makes it worse.** Holding yourself stiffly can make you feel more unsteady.
- **The fear loop.** Fear of dizziness keeps your nervous system watching for it.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause dizziness every day?** Yes, it can. If it lasts, see a doctor to check for PPPD and other causes.

**How is PPPD treated?** Vestibular rehabilitation, some medications, and therapy help many people.

**Why do I feel dizzy in stores?** Busy, moving visual scenes can overwhelm a balance system that's on alert.
