You made it through the hard week. The big project is done. The holidays are over. Vacation finally starts.

And then you crash. You're exhausted, you get a headache, or you come down with a cold right when you finally get to rest.

It can feel so unfair. But it's a very common pattern, and there's a reason for it.

## Your body was holding it together

During stressful stretches, your nervous system goes into high gear. Stress hormones help you push through, stay alert, and keep going, even when you're running on empty.

As long as the stress is there, your body keeps holding things together. But when the stress finally stops, your body lets go all at once. Everything you've been pushing through catches up with you.

Some people call this the "let-down effect."

## Signs you're living in this pattern

- You get sick at the start of every vacation
- You crash on weekends after a busy week
- You feel fine during a crisis and fall apart afterward
- You get headaches or migraines once things calm down
- You can't rest without feeling worse at first

## Why your body may not trust rest

For some people, the crash is a sign that their nervous system has learned to only let go when it absolutely has to. If you grew up needing to stay strong, stay busy, or keep everything running, your body may have learned that rest is only safe once everything else is handled.

That means your body may be living in a cycle: push, hold, crash, repeat. You can read more about [why you can't relax](/articles/why-cant-i-relax) and [why you're always tired](/articles/why-am-i-always-tired-nervous-system).

Talk to your doctor if you get sick often or the crashes are severe, so other causes can be checked.
