Can childhood trauma cause OCD? Trauma isn't the only cause of OCD, but research links them. A meta-analysis found that people who went through past trauma tended to have more severe obsessive-compulsive symptoms, especially after emotional abuse, sexual abuse, violence, or neglect. OCD is complex, and genetics and brain differences play a big role too. But for many people, compulsions grew out of a time when they needed to feel safe or in control.

You might check, clean, count, or repeat things until it feels "right," get stuck on frightening thoughts, or feel that something bad will happen if you don't do your rituals.

## Get support

OCD is very treatable. Please talk with a doctor or therapist who specializes in OCD. Exposure and response prevention (ERP) and some medications help many people. If you have thoughts of harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US.

## What research shows

A 2017 meta-analysis in the *Journal of Anxiety Disorders* combined 24 studies with more than 4,500 people. Past trauma was linked to more severe obsessive-compulsive spectrum symptoms. Four kinds of interpersonal trauma, violence, emotional abuse, sexual abuse, and neglect, were each linked to symptom severity. Trauma was more strongly linked to compulsions than to obsessions.

## Why trauma and OCD may connect

When a child lives with unpredictability or danger, their nervous system looks for ways to feel safe. Checking, ordering, or repeating can bring a moment of relief. Over time, the brain learns that the ritual is what keeps danger away, even when the danger is long gone.

## Signs your OCD may be tied to old fear

- Your rituals feel like keeping someone safe
- Symptoms started or got worse after a hard time
- You feel calmer only when everything is controlled
- The fear underneath feels much older than you

## Common questions

**Can trauma cause OCD in adults?** OCD can start or worsen after trauma at any age.

**Is trauma-related OCD different?** It's still OCD. Some people find that working on both the OCD and the trauma helps most.

**Can OCD go away?** Many people get much better with the right treatment.
