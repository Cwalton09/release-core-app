Can anxiety cause tingling in your hands and feet? Yes. During anxiety or panic, many people breathe faster without realizing it. That lowers carbon dioxide in the blood, which can make nerves more excitable and cause tingling or numbness in the hands, feet, and around the mouth. Stress also tightens blood vessels and muscles. But tingling and numbness can have medical causes too, so it's important to get checked.

It can be scary to feel your hands go numb or your fingers buzz, especially in the middle of a stressful moment.

## Call 911 if…

Call 911 right away if numbness or tingling is sudden and on one side of your body, or comes with face drooping, arm or leg weakness, confusion, trouble speaking, vision loss, or a severe headache. These can be signs of a stroke.

## Get it checked

Please see a doctor if tingling or numbness is ongoing, keeps coming back, or happens without stress. Common medical causes include:

- B12 deficiency
- Diabetes or high blood sugar
- Thyroid problems
- Pinched nerves or carpal tunnel
- Nerve conditions like neuropathy or MS
- Some medications

## How anxiety causes tingling

**Fast breathing.** When your nervous system senses danger, your breathing speeds up. If you breathe out more carbon dioxide than your body needs, the chemistry of your blood shifts, and nerves fire more easily. That's the pins-and-needles feeling, often in the fingers, hands, feet, and lips.

**Tight blood vessels.** Fight or flight sends blood to your big muscles and away from your hands and feet, which can make them feel cold, numb, or tingly.

**Muscle tension.** Tight shoulders, neck, and arms can press on nerves.

**More attention.** A nervous system on alert notices every sensation, which can make tingling feel bigger. See [can't take a deep breath?](/articles/why-cant-i-take-a-deep-breath)

## Signs it may be anxiety-related

- It happens during stress, worry, or panic
- It's in both hands or feet, not just one side
- It comes with a racing heart, fast breathing, or dizziness
- It fades as you calm down
- Your doctor hasn't found another cause

## Common questions

**Can anxiety make your hands go numb?** Yes, especially during panic or fast breathing. Ongoing numbness should be checked.

**Why do my hands tingle when I'm stressed?** Fast breathing and tightened blood vessels can cause tingling in your hands.

**Can anxiety cause tingling all over?** Some people feel it in many places, including the face and scalp. Rule out medical causes with your doctor.
