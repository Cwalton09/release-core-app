Can stress cause gray hair? Research suggests it can. In a 2020 Harvard study in *Nature*, stress in mice switched on the fight-or-flight nerves, which used up the stem cells that make hair color. A 2021 study of human hairs found that graying can line up with stressful periods, and that some gray hairs regained their color when stress eased. Genetics and age are still the main reasons hair turns gray, but your nervous system plays a part.

Many people notice a wave of gray after a hard year, a loss, or a long period of pressure.

## When to see a doctor

Graying is usually a normal part of aging. But early or sudden graying can sometimes be linked to thyroid problems, low B12, or other conditions. If you're graying very early, or it comes with other symptoms like fatigue or hair loss, ask your doctor about checking.

## What research shows

**The Harvard mouse study.** Researchers found that stress activated the sympathetic nerves, the fight-or-flight side of the nervous system, that connect to hair follicles. Those nerves released noradrenaline, which made the pigment stem cells activate all at once and get used up. Once they were gone, new hair grew in gray. The study was in mice, so it shows a mechanism, not proof of exactly what happens in people.

**The Columbia human hair study.** In a 2021 study in *eLife*, researchers mapped color along single human hairs, which act like a timeline of the past months. They found gray hairs that naturally regained their color, and some changes in color that lined up with stressful periods in people's lives. The study was small, but it suggests graying isn't always permanent, at least early on.

## How your nervous system is involved

Your hair follicles are connected to your nervous system. When fight-or-flight stays switched on, it affects blood flow, hormones, and stem cells throughout your body, including in your hair. ## Common questions

**Can gray hair from stress turn back?** The 2021 study found some gray hairs regained color when stress eased, likely only for hairs that recently turned gray. Hair that's been gray for a long time usually stays gray.

**Can stress turn your hair white overnight?** There's no good evidence for hair turning white overnight. Graying happens as new hair grows in.

**How do I stop stress graying?** Genetics matter most. Lowering long-term stress, sleeping well, and checking for vitamin deficiencies are reasonable steps.
