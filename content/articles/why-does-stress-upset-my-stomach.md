A big meeting. A hard conversation. A text you weren't expecting. And suddenly your stomach is in knots, or you're running for the bathroom.

If your stomach seems to react to stress before you even realize you're stressed, you're not imagining it.

## Your stomach feels stress first

Your gut is packed with nerves and wired to your brain. When your nervous system senses danger, your gut is one of the first places to respond. It can clench, cramp, rush, or shut down.

That's why stress can show up as stomach pain before it shows up as a worried thought.

## Stress and IBS

Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) is one of the most common digestive conditions. Researchers describe it as a problem with how the gut and brain communicate, and stress is known to be involved in it.

Many people with IBS notice their symptoms flare during stressful weeks, big life changes, or seasons when they feel unsafe or overwhelmed. Some notice they react to food differently depending on how stressed they are when they eat.

## A gut that's on high alert

When your nervous system has been on alert for a long time, your gut can become more sensitive too. Normal digestion, like gas or stretching, can start to feel painful or urgent.

Researchers have linked lower vagal tone, which is part of your body's calming system, with greater pain sensitivity in conditions like IBS. In simple terms, a nervous system that has a hard time settling can turn the volume up on what your gut feels. You can read more about [the gut-brain connection](/articles/gut-brain-connection-nervous-system).

## Signs stress is part of your stomach trouble

- Your symptoms flare before events, travel, or conflict
- They get worse during hard seasons of life
- They ease on calm days or vacations
- You feel your stomach react before you notice a thought
- Your gut seems more sensitive the more anxious you are

Please talk to your doctor about ongoing stomach symptoms so other causes can be checked. Get care right away for blood in your stool, unexplained weight loss, a fever, or severe pain.
