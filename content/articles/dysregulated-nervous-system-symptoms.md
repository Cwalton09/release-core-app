You hear the phrase "dysregulated nervous system" everywhere now. But what does it actually mean? And how do you know if it's you?

In simple terms, a dysregulated nervous system is one that has a hard time moving between alert and calm. It gets stuck on high, stuck on low, or swings back and forth between the two.

## What a regulated nervous system looks like

A regulated nervous system isn't one that's always calm. It's one that can respond to stress and then come back down.

Something hard happens, you feel it, you handle it, and afterward your body settles. You can feel stressed and still find your way back to okay.

When your system is dysregulated, that "coming back down" part is hard. Your body stays in protection mode long after the stress has passed.

## Emotional signs

- Feeling anxious, on edge, or irritable much of the time
- Big reactions to small things
- Going numb, flat, or disconnected
- Swinging between overwhelmed and shut down
- Trouble relaxing, even when nothing is wrong
- Feeling like you're always bracing for something
- Overthinking and replaying things over and over

## Physical signs

A dysregulated nervous system often shows up in the body too:

- Tight shoulders, jaw, or chest
- A racing heart or shallow breathing
- Stomach trouble when you're stressed
- Feeling wired and exhausted at the same time
- Trouble falling asleep or staying asleep
- Feeling foggy or having a hard time focusing
- Being extra sensitive to noise, light, or crowds

These can also have other causes, so it's always worth checking in with a doctor about physical symptoms. A nervous system pattern can exist alongside what's happening physically, not instead of it.

## What causes it

Dysregulation usually develops when your body has had to protect you for a long time.

That might come from a hard childhood, a stressful relationship, a long season of pressure, illness, loss, or simply carrying too much for too long. Your nervous system adapts to what it's been through. If it learned that the world isn't safe, it stays ready.
