Why can I feel my heartbeat all the time? Feeling your heartbeat is often normal, especially when you're lying down, it's quiet, or your heart is beating harder from stress, caffeine, or exercise. Anxiety makes it more noticeable in two ways: adrenaline makes your heart beat faster and stronger, and an anxious mind keeps checking the body for danger. Interestingly, research shows anxious people aren't actually better at sensing their heartbeat. They just pay much more attention to it.

You might feel your heart thumping in your chest at night, hear it in your ear on the pillow, or feel it pulsing in your stomach or throat.

## See a doctor if…

Please see a doctor if your heartbeat feels irregular, skips or flutters often, races at rest, or comes with dizziness, shortness of breath, or fainting. Thyroid problems, anemia, dehydration, pregnancy, and some medications can make your heartbeat stronger. Call 911 for chest pain, fainting, or a racing heart with shortness of breath.

## Why you notice your heartbeat

- **Lying down.** On your side or back, your heartbeat travels to your ear, neck, or chest more easily.
- **Quiet and stillness.** With fewer distractions, you notice inner sensations.
- **Adrenaline.** Stress makes your heart pump harder and faster.
- **Attention.** Once you notice it, your brain keeps tuning in, and worry makes it louder.

## What research shows

A 2022 meta-analysis in *Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews* looked at 55 studies and found no link between anxiety and how accurately people can count or sense their heartbeat. In other words, anxious people don't have better "heartbeat radar." Other research suggests the difference is in how much attention people pay to body sensations and how threatening they seem.

That's good news. The heartbeat itself is usually normal. What changes is how alarming it feels.

## Signs anxiety is behind it

- You notice it most when you're worried, still, or trying to sleep
- It fades when you're busy or distracted
- Your doctor's checks were normal
- Noticing it makes you more anxious

## Common questions

**Is it normal to feel your heartbeat in your stomach?** Yes, often. The aorta runs through your belly, and you can feel its pulse, especially when lying down.

**Why do I hear my heartbeat in my ear?** Lying on your ear makes the pulse in nearby blood vessels easier to hear. A constant pulsing sound in your ear should be checked by a doctor.

**How do I stop focusing on my heartbeat?** Shift your attention outward, slow your breathing with a longer exhale, and remind yourself that a heartbeat is a sign your body is working.
