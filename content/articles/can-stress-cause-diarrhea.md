Can stress cause diarrhea? Yes. When your nervous system senses stress, it releases hormones that speed up your colon and make your gut more sensitive. That's why you might need the bathroom urgently before a big meeting, a trip, or a hard conversation. Your body is getting ready to fight or run, and emptying the gut is part of that old survival response.

It can be embarrassing and disruptive. You plan your day around bathrooms, or you skip things because you're afraid your stomach will act up.

## Your gut has a direct line to your brain

Your gut is lined with millions of nerve cells and is wired straight to your brain, mostly through the vagus nerve. When you're stressed, your brain releases a stress hormone called CRF. Research shows CRF slows down the stomach but speeds up the colon. That's the urgent, loose feeling so many people know.

Stress also changes how sensitive your gut is, so normal digestion can feel crampy and urgent. You can read more in [the gut-brain connection](/articles/gut-brain-connection-nervous-system).

## Why it happens when you're anxious

In a real emergency, your body wants to be light and ready to move. Clearing the gut is one way it does that. Your nervous system can't tell the difference between running from danger and walking into a stressful meeting. It responds the same way.

## Signs your diarrhea may be stress-related

- It happens before events, travel, or hard conversations
- It flares during stressful weeks
- It comes with a racing mind, a knot in your stomach, or nausea
- It eases on calm days or vacations
- Your medical tests haven't found another cause

Diarrhea can also come from infections, food intolerances, IBS, celiac disease, and other conditions. Please see a doctor if it lasts more than a couple of days, keeps coming back, or comes with blood, fever, severe pain, dehydration, or weight loss.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause diarrhea every day?** It can, especially when stress is ongoing. Daily diarrhea should still be checked by a doctor. See also [IBS and the nervous system](/articles/ibs-and-the-nervous-system).

**Why do I get diarrhea when I'm nervous?** Stress hormones speed up your colon. It's part of your body's fight-or-flight response.

**How long does stress diarrhea last?** It often eases once the stressful moment passes, but it can linger when stress is constant.

## Why it keeps coming back

If your gut reacts every time life gets stressful, your nervous system is still treating those moments as danger.
