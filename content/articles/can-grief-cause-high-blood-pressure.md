Can grief cause high blood pressure? Grief can raise blood pressure and heart rate, especially in the first weeks after a loss. Research has found higher blood pressure and heart rate in recently bereaved people, which tended to come down over the following months. Grief is one of the biggest stresses your nervous system can face, and your heart feels it.

You might notice higher readings at the doctor's office, a pounding heart, chest tightness, or headaches since the loss.

## See a doctor if…

Call 911 for chest pain, pressure, or tightness, especially with shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, or pain spreading to your arm, jaw, or back. Please see a doctor if your blood pressure is high, or if you have heart disease or risk factors and you've recently lost someone. Keep taking any medications you've been prescribed. If grief feels unbearable, call or text 988 in the US.

## What research shows

A study in *Heart, Lung and Circulation* followed 80 bereaved people and 80 people who weren't grieving. In the first two weeks after a loss, the bereaved group had higher systolic blood pressure and heart rate. By six months, these had come down.

A larger study in *Circulation* found that the risk of a heart attack was about 21 times higher in the 24 hours after the death of someone close, and it dropped each day after. The absolute risk is still small, but it's highest for people who already have heart risk.

## Why grief affects your heart

When you lose someone, your stress system switches on hard. Adrenaline and cortisol rise, your heart beats faster, your blood vessels tighten, and sleep often breaks down. For most people, this eases with time. See [can stress cause high blood pressure](/articles/can-stress-cause-high-blood-pressure).

## Looking after your heart while grieving

- Keep taking your medications
- Check your blood pressure if you can
- Eat and sleep as well as you're able
- Let others help
- Tell your doctor about the loss

## Common questions

**Is broken heart syndrome real?** Yes. Takotsubo syndrome is a real heart condition that can follow intense emotional stress. It needs emergency care.

**How long does grief raise blood pressure?** In research, it was highest in the first weeks and tended to come down by six months.

**Can grief cause heart palpitations?** Yes. A racing or pounding heart is common in grief. Get it checked if it's new or worrying.
