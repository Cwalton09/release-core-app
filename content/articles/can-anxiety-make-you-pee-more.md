Can anxiety make you pee more? Yes, it can. Your bladder is controlled by your nervous system, and when you're anxious, your body is on high alert, which can make the bladder feel full sooner and create urgency. Research has found that people with anxiety are more likely to have overactive bladder symptoms like frequent urination, urgency, and waking up at night to pee.

Right before a meeting, a date, or a long drive, you suddenly need to go. Again.

## What research shows

In a 2023 study of more than 1,000 people with bladder symptoms, anxiety was linked to overactive bladder in men and to waking up at night to pee in women. Other studies have found that around half of people with overactive bladder also have anxiety symptoms, and that more anxiety goes along with worse bladder symptoms.

These studies show a strong link. The relationship likely goes both ways: anxiety can worsen bladder symptoms, and bladder symptoms can make people anxious.

## How anxiety affects your bladder

- **Your nervous system controls the bladder**, so stress signals can change how it behaves
- **Muscle tension** in the pelvic floor and abdomen can press on the bladder
- **Heightened body awareness** makes you notice bladder sensations sooner
- **Poor sleep** from anxiety means more nighttime trips
- **Caffeine and fluids** people use to cope can add to it

## Nervous pee before big moments

Needing to pee right before something stressful is very common. Your body is preparing for action, and your nervous system is turned all the way up. See also [signs your nervous system is stuck in fight or flight](/articles/signs-nervous-system-stuck-in-fight-or-flight).

## When to see a doctor

Frequent urination can also be a sign of a urinary tract infection, diabetes, prostate problems, pregnancy, or other conditions. Please see a doctor if it's new, comes with pain or burning, blood in your urine, fever, increased thirst, or if it's affecting your daily life.

## Common questions

**Why do I have to pee when I'm nervous?** Your nervous system is on alert, which can make your bladder feel full and urgent.

**Can anxiety cause frequent urination at night?** It can. Research links anxiety with waking up to pee, especially in women.

**Can stress cause an overactive bladder?** Stress and anxiety are strongly linked with overactive bladder symptoms.
