Can stress cause acne? Stress can make acne worse. In a study of university students, acne got more severe during exams, and the more stressed students felt, the worse their skin got, even after accounting for sleep and diet. Stress hormones can increase oil production and inflammation in the skin, which gives breakouts more fuel.

You have a big event coming up, and right on schedule, your skin breaks out. Or you've been under pressure for weeks, and your face shows it.

## What research shows

In a 2003 study by researchers including Alexa Kimball, university students with acne were checked during exams and during calmer times. Their acne was worse during exams, and the increase tracked closely with how stressed they felt. The link held even after accounting for changes in sleep and diet.

It was a small study, and it shows a link rather than proof. But it matches what so many people see in the mirror.

## How stress affects your skin

When your nervous system is under stress:

- **Stress hormones** like cortisol, along with other hormones, can increase oil production
- **Inflammation** rises, making breakouts redder and slower to heal
- **Skin barrier and healing** can slow down
- **Habits change**: less sleep, more touching or picking, different eating

Your skin also has its own stress response system, with nerve endings and receptors for stress hormones. That's part of why skin is so reactive to stress. See also [stress and skin flare-ups](/articles/stress-skin-flare-ups-eczema-psoriasis).

## Stress acne vs. hormonal acne

- **Stress acne** often flares during or right after stressful times, can show up anywhere, and settles as stress settles.
- **Hormonal acne** often follows your cycle and tends to show up along the jaw and chin.

The two overlap, because stress also affects your hormones. If acne is severe, painful, scarring, or not improving, please see a dermatologist.

## Common questions

**Where does stress acne show up?** It can appear anywhere, often on the face, chest, or back. Many people notice it on the forehead or jaw.

**How long does stress acne last?** It usually improves as stress settles, though healing can take a few weeks.

**Can anxiety make you break out?** Yes. Anxiety and stress can trigger or worsen breakouts.
