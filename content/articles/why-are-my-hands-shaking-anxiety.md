Why are your hands shaking? Often because your nervous system is in fight-or-flight. Everyone has a tiny tremor all the time, too small to notice. When you're anxious or stressed, adrenaline makes that natural tremor stronger, so your hands visibly shake. Doctors call this an enhanced physiologic tremor. Caffeine, low blood sugar, and lack of sleep can make it worse.

You go to sign your name, hold a cup, or give a presentation, and your hands won't stop trembling.

## Why anxiety makes you shake

When your body senses a threat, it releases adrenaline to get you ready to fight or run. Your muscles tense and get primed for action. That extra charge makes the small, natural tremor everyone has much easier to see.

Doctors note that enhanced physiologic tremor is one of the most common tremors seen in primary care, and that anxiety, caffeine, fatigue, and some medications can bring it on.

## Other things that can make hands shake

- **Caffeine** and energy drinks
- **Low blood sugar**, especially if you skipped a meal
- **Poor sleep** or exhaustion
- **Some medications**, like asthma inhalers or certain antidepressants
- **Alcohol withdrawal**
- **Thyroid problems**, especially an overactive thyroid

## Shaking after stress is over

Some people shake after the stressful moment has passed. That can be your body releasing the extra energy it built up. See also [why do I crash after stress?](/articles/why-do-i-crash-after-stress)

## When to see a doctor

Please see a doctor if your shaking is new, getting worse, happens even when you're calm, affects one side of the body, or comes with other symptoms like weakness, trouble walking, weight loss, or a racing heart at rest. Conditions like essential tremor, thyroid problems, and Parkinson's disease can also cause tremor and need medical care.

## Common questions

**Can anxiety cause shaking all day?** If your nervous system is on alert all day, you may notice shakiness on and off.

**Why do my hands shake when I'm nervous but not other times?** Adrenaline from nerves strengthens your body's natural tremor.

**Why does my body feel shaky inside?** Many people feel an inner trembling with anxiety. See [why does my body feel like it's vibrating?](/articles/why-does-my-body-feel-like-its-vibrating)

## Why it keeps coming back

If your hands keep shaking in stressful moments, your nervous system is still treating those moments as danger.

The real root is often very different from what you think. It usually isn't the presentation or the people watching. It's an old rule your body learned long before, like *if I mess up, I won't be safe* or *I have to get this perfect*, and today's moment is just turning up its volume.
