Can stress make you sick? It can make you more likely to get sick. Stress doesn't create a virus, but it can lower your body's defenses. In a well-known study, people who reported more stress were more likely to become infected and develop a cold after being exposed to a cold virus. When your nervous system stays on alert, your immune system works differently.

You might catch every bug going around, get sick right after a deadline, or feel like you're always fighting something off.

## See a doctor if…

Please see a doctor if you get sick very often, if infections last a long time or keep coming back, or if you have a high fever, trouble breathing, unexplained weight loss, or night sweats. Frequent infections can have medical causes that need checking.

## What research shows

A study published in the *New England Journal of Medicine* gave 394 healthy volunteers nasal drops containing a cold virus, then watched who got sick. The more stress people reported, the more likely they were to become infected and develop a cold. Colds ranged from about 27% in the least stressed people to 47% in the most stressed. Smoking, sleep, diet, and exercise didn't explain the difference.

## How stress lowers your defenses

When stress hormones stay high for a long time, they change how your immune system responds. Your body puts its energy into getting through the threat, and fighting off germs gets less attention. Poor sleep and running on empty add to it. See [does stress weaken your immune system](/articles/does-stress-weaken-your-immune-system).

## Common signs stress is wearing you down

- Getting sick after busy or emotional stretches
- Colds that hang on longer than they should
- Cold sores or other flare-ups during stress
- Feeling run down most of the time
- Getting sick as soon as you finally rest

## Common questions

**Can stress make you physically sick?** Yes. It can make you more likely to catch infections and can cause physical symptoms like headaches, stomach upset, and fatigue.

**Why do I get sick after stress is over?** When the pressure drops, your body finally lets down its guard, and symptoms that were held back can show up.

**How do I stop getting sick from stress?** Sleep, nourishing food, and real rest help. Calming your nervous system is the deeper part.
