Can stress raise cholesterol? Research suggests it can. Cholesterol rises temporarily during stress, and one study found that people whose cholesterol rose the most during a short stressful task were more likely to have high cholesterol three years later. Stress hormones affect how your body makes and uses fat for energy. Genetics, diet, and activity still matter a lot.

You might notice your numbers creeping up during a hard season, or be confused why your cholesterol is high even though you eat well.

## See your doctor

High cholesterol usually has no symptoms, so the only way to know is a blood test. Please keep up with checkups and take any cholesterol medication as prescribed. Talk with your doctor about your overall heart risk, including blood pressure, blood sugar, family history, and smoking.

## What research shows

A study in *Health Psychology* gave 199 middle-aged adults short, moderately stressful tasks in a lab. Total cholesterol and LDL cholesterol rose during stress. Three years later, people whose cholesterol had risen most during the stress tasks had higher cholesterol levels and were more likely to have clinically high cholesterol, even after accounting for weight change, smoking, alcohol, and their starting levels.

## How stress affects cholesterol

- **Energy for fight-or-flight.** Stress hormones release fat into your blood for quick energy.
- **The liver responds.** Cortisol affects how your liver makes cholesterol.
- **Habits shift.** Stress can change eating, sleep, and movement.
- **Repeated spikes add up.** A body that reacts strongly to stress over and over may keep levels higher.

## What helps

- Regular movement
- Fiber-rich food, like oats, beans, and vegetables
- Good sleep
- Not smoking
- Calming your stress response

## Common questions

**Can anxiety raise cholesterol?** Long-term stress and anxiety can affect cholesterol, along with many other factors.

**How fast can stress raise cholesterol?** It rises temporarily during stress. The bigger question is what happens over years of repeated stress.

**Can lowering stress lower cholesterol?** It may help alongside diet, exercise, and any medication your doctor recommends.
