Can stress cause blurry vision? Yes, stress and anxiety can cause short-term blurry vision in some people. Adrenaline widens your pupils and changes how your eyes focus, and stress often comes with eye strain, dry eyes, poor sleep, and tense muscles. Long-term stress and high cortisol are also linked to central serous chorioretinopathy, an eye condition where fluid builds up under the retina and blurs vision.

You might notice it during a panic attack, after long days of stress at a screen, or when you're exhausted. Things look fuzzy, bright light bothers you, or your eyes feel tired and hard to focus.

## Get emergency help if…

Sudden vision changes can be serious. Go to an emergency room or call 911 for sudden blurry or lost vision, double vision, flashes of light, a curtain over part of your vision, eye pain, or vision changes with a severe headache, weakness, numbness, confusion, or trouble speaking. These can be signs of stroke or a serious eye problem.

See an eye doctor for blurry vision that lasts, keeps coming back, or affects one eye.

## How stress affects your eyes

In fight-or-flight, adrenaline makes your pupils bigger so you can scan for danger. This lets in more light and can make things look less sharp or too bright. Stress can also make you blink less, which dries out your eyes, and tense face and neck muscles can add to eye strain and headaches.

## What research shows

Central serous chorioretinopathy (CSC) is an eye condition where fluid collects under the retina, often causing blurry or distorted vision in one eye. A 2010 review in the journal *Eye* notes that CSC happens with both the body's own high cortisol and steroid medications. The authors suggest doctors talk with patients about a possible link to stress.

A 2020 review of CSC biomarkers describes the stress and cortisol link as likely but still being studied. The research is mostly observational, so it shows a link rather than proof.

## Signs stress may be part of your blurry vision

- It comes and goes with stress or anxiety
- It's worse after long, tense days or poor sleep
- Your eye exam was normal
- It comes with tight shoulders, a tense jaw, or headaches

## Common questions

**Can anxiety make your vision blurry?** Yes, especially during a panic attack, when adrenaline peaks. It usually clears as you calm down.

**How long does stress blurry vision last?** Blurriness from adrenaline or eye strain often clears within minutes to hours. If it lasts, see an eye doctor.

**Can stress damage your eyes?** Most stress-related blurriness is temporary. CSC can affect vision and needs an eye doctor's care, though it often clears on its own.
