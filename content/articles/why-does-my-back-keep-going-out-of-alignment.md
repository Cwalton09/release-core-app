Why does my back keep going out after the chiropractor? One common reason is that tight muscles and fascia keep pulling your body back out of position. And when that tension keeps returning, it's often because your nervous system is stuck in fight or flight.

You get adjusted. You feel lined up and free. You walk out standing taller.

And a few days later, it's out again. Same spot. Same rib. Same ache. Back on the table.

If you've been through that cycle more times than you can count, you're not doing anything wrong, and your chiropractor isn't either. Something keeps pulling you back out.

## What keeps pulling you out of place

Your bones don't move on their own. They're held in position by muscles, tendons, and fascia, the connective tissue that wraps through your whole body.

When those tissues are balanced, an adjustment can hold. But when certain areas are tight and pulling, they can tug your spine or ribs right back to where they were.

So the question isn't only "why does my back go out?" It's "why does my body keep tightening in a way that pulls it out?"

## Fight or flight keeps your body braced

When your nervous system is in fight or flight, your body gets ready for action. Your shoulders rise, your back and neck tighten, your breathing gets shallow and high in your chest, and your core locks.

That's helpful for a few minutes in a real emergency. But a lot of people live there for years. And a body that's braced all the time keeps pulling on the same places, day after day.

That's why an adjustment can feel amazing and still not last. It puts things back where they belong. Then your nervous system goes right back to bracing, and the tension pulls them out again. You can read more about [the signs your nervous system is stuck in fight or flight](/articles/signs-nervous-system-stuck-in-fight-or-flight).

## Ribs that keep slipping

Ribs are attached to your spine and wrapped in muscles you breathe with. When your body is braced, breathing tends to get shallow and tight, and the muscles around your ribs stay on guard.

In my experience, people whose ribs keep "going out" very often have a nervous system that hasn't let go of something. The rib is where they feel it. The bracing is what keeps causing it.

## Signs your nervous system is part of it

- Adjustments feel great but don't hold for long
- It goes out during stressful weeks, conflict, or big changes
- You hold tension in your neck, shoulders, or jaw most of the time
- Your breathing feels shallow or you can't get a full breath
- You feel on edge or hard to settle, even when nothing is wrong

Back pain, rib pain, and alignment problems can have many physical causes, so please keep working with your chiropractor, doctor, or physical therapist. Nervous system work doesn't replace that care. It can help the care you're already getting hold.

## Common questions

**Why won't my chiropractic adjustments hold?** Adjustments are held in place by the muscles and fascia around the joint. If those tissues keep tightening and pulling, the joint can be pulled back out. Ongoing stress and a nervous system on alert are common reasons that tension keeps returning.

**Can stress make your back go out of alignment?** Stress tightens muscles and fascia, especially in the back, neck, shoulders, and ribs. Many people notice their back goes out more during stressful times.

**Why do my ribs keep going out of place?** Tight muscles around the ribs, shallow chest breathing, and a body that's braced for long periods can all keep pulling on the ribs. If it keeps happening, have it checked, and look at whether your nervous system ever fully settles.
