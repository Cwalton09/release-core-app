Can burnout cause physical symptoms? Yes. Burnout isn't just feeling tired of work. A review of prospective studies found that burnout predicted physical problems like heart disease, chronic pain, long-lasting fatigue, headaches, stomach and digestive problems, and breathing problems, along with insomnia and depression. When your nervous system runs on stress for too long, your body starts paying the price.

You might wake up exhausted, get every cold, have headaches most days, feel your stomach in knots, or ache in your neck and back.

## See a doctor if…

Burnout symptoms overlap with many medical conditions, like thyroid problems, anemia, sleep apnea, and depression. Please see a doctor for ongoing fatigue, chest pain, weight changes, or symptoms that keep getting worse. If you feel hopeless or think about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US.

## What research shows

A 2017 systematic review in *PLoS One* looked at prospective studies, which follow people over time, on job burnout. Burnout predicted:

- **Physical:** high cholesterol, type 2 diabetes, heart disease, chronic pain, prolonged fatigue, headaches, digestive problems, breathing problems, and injuries
- **Psychological:** insomnia, depressive symptoms, and more use of medications
- **Work:** more sick days and disability

## How burnout wears down your body

Burnout is what happens when your nervous system stays in fight-or-flight for months, then starts to crash. Stress hormones stay high, sleep suffers, inflammation rises, and your immune system weakens. Eventually your body may swing into shutdown: heavy, foggy, and unable to recover.

## Common physical signs of burnout

- Exhaustion that rest doesn't fix
- Frequent headaches
- Stomach problems, nausea, or IBS flares
- Getting sick often
- Aches, tight neck and shoulders
- Racing heart or chest tightness
- Trouble sleeping, even when exhausted
- Brain fog. See [burnout brain fog](/articles/burnout-brain-fog).

## Common questions

**What are the first physical signs of burnout?** Often poor sleep, constant tiredness, headaches, and getting sick more often.

**How long does it take to recover from burnout?** It varies. Many people need weeks to months, and real recovery usually means changing what keeps the stress going.

**Can burnout cause permanent damage?** Most effects improve with rest and recovery. Long-term burnout is linked to health risks, which is why it's worth taking seriously.
