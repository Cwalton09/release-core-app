# Runway Prompts: Background Clips for Upcoming Posts

Each clip goes **behind the words** on a Reel or TikTok. I add the text, the captions, and the keyword strip afterward. Your posts already go out every day, so the first batch will upgrade the posts that are already scheduled. Nothing extra gets added.

## Settings (the same every time)

- **Type:** Text to Video, using Runway's newest video model
- **Shape:** 9:16 (tall, for phones)
- **Length:** 10 seconds (or 5 if you're low on credits)
- **Never** ask for words or text in the video. AI garbles letters. I add the text myself.
- **Never** show faces or realistic people. Hands, silhouettes from behind, and nature are fine.

If a clip comes out weird or jumpy, just hit generate again. Pick the calmest, smoothest version.

## How to send them to me

Download each clip and save it into this folder on your computer:

`release core app/release-code-app-main/public/social/runway`

Name each file with the number below, for example `01-3am.mp4`. Then tell me "clips are in."
