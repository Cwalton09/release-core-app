# Talking video scripts: week 1

Each script is 20 to 40 seconds. Say it in your own words, it doesn't have to be exact. Start talking right away, no "hi guys." Post from the TikTok or Instagram app so you can add a trending sound at low volume behind your voice.

Best times for your own posts: 12 to 2 PM or 7 to 9 PM (your scheduled posts go out at 10 AM and 6 PM).

---

## 1. My money block (your story)

So every time I started getting ahead, something held me back. Not because I didn't want more, but because more felt like something I could lose.

I had no idea my body was holding that until I did a session on it. Since clearing it, manifesting has honestly become so easy.

If money keeps slipping away from you, it might not be your mindset. It might be what your body learned. Link in bio."

## Tips for recording

- Natural light facing you, phone at eye level.
- Record a few takes, post the one that feels most like you, not the most perfect.
- Add on-screen text for the hook, since most people watch with the sound off.
- Reply to comments in the first hour after posting.
