# Practitioner outreach: chiropractors, therapists, life coaches

Send these one at a time from your own email, to people you've looked up yourself. Add one specific line about their practice so it doesn't read like a mass email. 10 to 20 a day is plenty.

---

## Email

**Subject:** A tool your clients can use between sessions

Hi [first name],

I'm Chelsea Walton, founder of Release Core. I came across [practice name] and [one specific, honest thing: their focus, a post, their approach], and thought this might be a good fit for the people you work with.

Release Core is a nervous-system method and app that helps people find the belief underneath a reaction: the anxiety, tension, or pattern that keeps coming back even when they understand it. Clients ask their nervous system whether there is an emotional connection to what they're feeling, follow their body back to where it started, and rewire it with questions their body can say yes to.

[Pick the line that fits who you're emailing:]
- Chiropractors: It can be a good fit for patients whose tension keeps coming back after adjustments.
- Therapists: It's a between-session tool that complements your work, never a replacement for it.
- Life coaches: It helps clients find the block underneath the goal they keep circling.

We also have Release Core Pro (pro-release-core.com), a professional platform with client profiles, digital waivers, session tracking, and personalized nighttime scripts for your clients. You can see how it works here: release-core.com/practitioners

Would you be open to a quick look? I'd love to walk you through it so you can experience it yourself first.

Warmly,

## Where to find people

- Instagram and TikTok: search #chiropractor, #somatictherapist, #lifecoach, #traumainformedcoach and message people whose content already talks about the nervous system.
- LinkedIn: search "trauma-informed coach", "somatic therapist", or "chiropractor" plus your city.
- Psychology Today and coach directories: use each person's own contact form.
- Facebook groups for coaches and wellness practitioners: share the practitioner platform where group rules allow it.

## Rules

- Don't add these people to your Resend list or send them a broadcast. Resend's rules ban cold lists, and spam complaints could shut off the account that sends your customer and password-reset emails.
- Never say Release Core treats, heals, or cures anything. Therapists in particular need to hear that it supports their care rather than replacing it.
- If someone says no or doesn't reply after one follow-up, let it go.
