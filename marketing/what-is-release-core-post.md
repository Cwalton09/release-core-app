## Caption A

The anxiety. The exhaustion. The relationship you can't let go of. The symptom that showed up out of nowhere. 🤍

Most of the time, it isn't the real problem. It's your body following a rule it learned to keep you safe, like "I can't rest until everything is done" or "if I stop watching, I'll get blindsided." The situation that created the rule is long over, but your body is still running it.

That's what Release Core is for.

Instead of guessing, we ask your body. With a simple sway test, it answers yes or no and leads you to what's really driving it, when it started, and the exact rule it's still following. Then you rewire that rule so your body doesn't need the old protection anymore.

You leave with a summary of what came up and a nightly script so the new belief sticks.

Release Core doesn't stop at understanding why. In a session, your body shows you the exact rule it's still following, and you rewire it so it can finally let go.
