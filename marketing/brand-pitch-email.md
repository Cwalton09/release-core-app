## Tips

- Personalize the second paragraph for every brand. Generic pitches get ignored.
- Find the right person: look for a "Partnerships", "Influencer", or "Affiliate" page on the brand's site, or an email like partnerships@ or collabs@. You can also message the brand on Instagram.
- If they offer free product only at first, that's normal at this stage. Say yes only to brands you'd use anyway.
- Ask about their affiliate program too. It's an easy yes for brands and earns you a percentage of every sale.
- Always label paid or gifted posts with #ad or Instagram's "Paid partnership" tag.
- Never say a product treats, heals, or cures anything.
